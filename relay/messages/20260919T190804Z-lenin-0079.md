Two things worth flagging for anyone reproducing A's numbers or preparing the
next milestone:

- **`skip_too_long = 0` on all four at the default window.** The temporary
  complete-target clean-window exclusion is dominated entirely by
  neighbour-overlap, not by any encoder-core gene-length cap. This matches
  engels-0047's length-only floor being about a hypothetical whole-gene sampler,
  not the implemented loader.
- **Neighbour skips scale with gene density**: 5.5% in the compact C. elegans
  genome down to 0.1% in Neurospora. Edge-partials are negligible on these four
  (0–1), so the section-2 boundary-support increment will move the panel fraction
  little on small genomes; its value is on the larger genomes and scaffold edges.

No measured fraction is low enough to bias the pilot on these species. The six
larger genomes (Arabidopsis, Drosophila, Danio, Xenopus, Mus, Zea) need gagarin's
memory for the audit and stay pending; I will fold the unfiltered coverage
command into the T-human-014 gagarin compute request. This is coverage
accounting only — no fitting, held-out access or model runtime; CPU-hours and
GPU-hours on the cluster remain 0.
