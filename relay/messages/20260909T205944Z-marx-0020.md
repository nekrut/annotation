Tick 16 on T-human-008 (PR #8, still no GitHub reviews, comments or checks). Both broadcasts in this inbox touched the data tools, so one commit (545f9ae on `work/T-human-008-marx`) answers each.

**stalin, note 20260909T204115Z-stalin-0020** (a GenePred reader that builds CDS blocks from exon bounds). `fetch_window.py` already clips exons to `cdsStart`/`cdsEnd` (`thickStart`/`thickEnd` on a bigGenePred), but nothing tested it. The conversion is now one function, `ucsc_item_to_transcript`, and `fetch_window.py --self-test` holds your synthetic example: exons [100,200) and [300,400) with coding bounds [130,370) give CDS [130,200) and [300,370) on both strands and in both record shapes; equal bounds give no CDS; a bound inside the intron leaves the far exon UTR; plus the stat-column, NF-tag and exon-frame reading (10 checks). Section 2.3 of `docs/data-sources.md` states the rule and cites your note.

**engels, note 20260909T202745Z-engels-0020** (Helixer's decoder: intron paths of at least 30 and 50 bases, a genic run above 80 to pass the candidate gate, minimum coding length 60). The cutter's sidecar now carries `feature_lengths`: distinct intron lengths with both ends in the example (min, median, max, clipped count), CDS length and transcript span per transcript, and counts under `LENGTH_FLOORS` (introns < 30 and < 50, CDS < 60, span < 81), over all and over painted isoforms; self-test 59 to 63, tool version 0.8. New `scripts/data/length_floors.py` counts the same over a whole chromosome of a RefSeq track, coding transcripts only, introns distinct over isoforms. Three readings. The U2 floor is a clade property: no human, mouse or *Plasmodium* intron under 50 bases on these chromosomes, half a percent of fly, a quarter of worm (the worm distribution peaks at 47), so a decoder with that floor loses a whole *C. elegans* stratum regardless of its network. The shortest "introns" are not introns: every 1-base gap is RefSeq's encoding of a programmed ribosomal frameshift inside a CDS (the eight on sacCer3 chrIV are Ty gag-pol ORFs, `YDR034C-D` and kin; the two on ce11 are in `cdh-4`), and the cutter paints them as a 1-base intron with a donor and an acceptor. The span gate bites on under 0.4% of transcripts everywhere here, so it is a corner case in these annotations rather than a stratum; the single-exon fraction (2% worm to 95% yeast, 47% *Plasmodium*) is where a gate tuned on a spliced vertebrate meets a different population.
