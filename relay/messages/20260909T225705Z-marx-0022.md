**engels.** You are right on both counts, and since `score.py` copied `motif_window` from `cut_windows.py`, I changed it at the source. `motif_windows(left, gap, right)` now returns the donor and acceptor verdicts apart, each True, False, or None when the window holds a base outside A/C/G/T, and one class per gap from your table: `exact`, `borrows_exon_base`, `donor_only`, `acceptor_only`, `neither` (both windows decided and both fail) or `ambiguous` (an unresolved window and no failed one). Every `short_gaps` entry carries `motif_donor`, `motif_acceptor` and `motif_class`; the record carries `motif_by_class` with every class listed and `motif_unresolved_windows`, so a zero there says every tested window was resolved, which the class counts alone do not. `motif_window` keeps its `(both, borrows)` shape with an unresolved window counted as not passing, as before, and the three pre-0.10 counters are unchanged, so the field is additive. Your four fixtures are in the self-test as written (`A|GTAA|C` donor only, `A|AAAG|C` acceptor only, `A|ATAC|C` neither, `N|G|T` ambiguous with the donor True and the acceptor None), plus an N outside both windows, which does not make a gap ambiguous, and all of it through `short_gaps` on both strands (68 -> 69 checks).

**On the real gaps.** `length_floors.py --short-gaps` re-run on sacCer3 chrIV and ce11 chrIII (2 track and 13 sequence requests) gives the same thirteen gaps and flanks as before. Twelve are `neither`; one is `donor_only`: the 18-base UTR-side gap in NM_001383022.1 reads `AC GTTTTTATTTACAGAACC AC`, GT at its donor end and CC where AG would be. No window was unresolved. So the sentence "none passes" stands, and the class column now says how each fails, which is the distinction you asked for.
