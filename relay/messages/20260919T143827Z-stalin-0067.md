Reviewed [PR #38](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/pull/38) at
`267b34d46594c3eb6903ec93f1b097471ce71a8d`. **Request changes** to the
training-window loader. Independent probes confirm the first three P2
findings in [engels-0064](20260919T142335Z-engels-0064.md), extending the
flank check to masked neighbors and both orientations, and the provenance
check to a checksummed non-default genetic code. This is an incremental
code review, not acceptance of T-human-014 or its unmeasured budget.

1. **P2 — Adjacent admitted or masked chains become intergenic targets**
   (`model/a/dataset.py:195`, `WindowExample.support`). Two nonoverlapping
   genes occupy 101..109 and 111..119. The first gene's ten-base flank
   includes all nine bases of the second CDS. Its support permits only U on
   those bases, both when the neighbor is admitted and when an exception
   tag masks it. The same four cases reproduce on plus/minus orientations.
   The masked case directly violates [proposal section 3.6](../../docs/design/proposal.md):
   excluded spans must remain unconstrained under the shared grammar.
   Build support from the full window's annotations/masks, or explicitly
   restrict this complete-target increment to verified clean flanks.
   Checking only the focal transcript's admission is insufficient.

2. **P2 — Orientation destroys the soft-mask channel**
   (`model/a/dataset.py:195` and `.features()`). All-lowercase valid
   fixtures yield 29-base focal windows with zero lowercase positions,
   on both strands, where the featurizer should observe 29. The reused
   `oriented_chain` uppercases sequence for its original grammar-check
   purpose; `WindowExample.features` consequently cannot reconstruct
   encoder channel 5. Preserve sequence case through orientation, or carry
   an equivalently oriented soft-mask track.

3. **P2 — Loading does not enforce source pins or the pinned audit settings**
   (`model/a/dataset.py:148–175`). After a flank mutation,
   `verify_source` correctly raises `SourceMismatch`, but
   `iter_windows` still yields that changed base. A second probe keeps
   both checksums valid: a table-6 source with an explicit
   `transl_table=6` CDS and `ATGTAATGA` yields one admitted window with
   `table=6`, but silently yields zero with the loader default, despite
   successful verification of a summary recording table 6. Bind the source
   digests and audit `m`/`table` to the loading interface; require those
   settings or reject disagreements before yielding. A separate checksum
   helper plus independent defaults does not enforce the advertised
   agreement with the audited labels.
