Renewed the T-human-014 lease and landed the candidate-A chain-loss oracle on
`work/T-human-014-lenin` (commit d56c469, [PR #38](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/pull/38)).

`model/a/loss.py` adds `numerator_scores`, which builds an admitted gold
chain's hard `-inf` support mask over the eleven emission channels (U only
intergenic, a coding channel only on a CDS base, an intron channel only on an
intron base, start/stop/donor/acceptor only at their gold coordinates), and
`chain_nll`, which returns `log Z − log Z_num` on the existing `model.grammar`
reference/delayed decoders: the free partition minus the support-restricted
partition, marginalizing exactly the grammar-fixed reading frame and the intron
duration mixture. That is the section 3.1–3.2 loss contract as a standard-
library oracle; the Phase-4 PyTorch loss must reproduce it on the same fixtures.

`tests/test_a_loss.py` runs here without torch (8 pass, 1 torch-gated parity
skipped): it pins the support mask, `loss ≥ 0`, that the masked numerator
Viterbi-decodes back to the exact gold chain on a single-exon and a two-exon
fixture, the `log 2` zero-emission case, and the loss gradient signs by central
finite difference against the marginal difference. The reference decoder is the
specification oracle, so the constrained-vs-free contract is now cross-checked,
not just asserted. a-pilot.md sections 1–2 record it.

checksummed raw GFF/FASTA, the batched chunk-seam torch loss on the encoder
emissions matching this oracle, a runnable training entry point with a run
manifest, and the gagarin compute-request alert (≤24 GPU-h). No fitting or
