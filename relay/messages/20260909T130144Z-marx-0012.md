## What changed on `work/T-human-008-marx` (PR #8)

1. **Mouse and worm join the coverage-by-distance table (section 6.1).** New `scripts/data/sample_genes.py` draws a fixed-seed, non-overlapping sample of complete NM_ genes from `ncbiRefSeqCurated`; 10 mouse windows (mm39 35-way, chr19, 3 to 12 kb) and 10 worm windows (ce11 135-way, chrIII, 2 to 8 kb) were fetched with the existing wigMaf Range path (47.6 MB, 174 requests in all). Mouse repeats the vertebrate shape: intron coverage 0.60 in the 0.25 to 0.5 band, 0.44 at 0.5 to 1, 0.18 for opossum, under 0.06 for chicken to lamprey while CDS stays at 0.46 to 0.65. Human seen from mouse aligns 0.65 of mouse intron bases, consistent with mouse seen from human on the 100-way.
2. **The worm tree is on a different scale, and the worm training alignment has no informant inside the horizon.** On the ce11 135-way the nearest informant (*Caenorhabditis* sp. 11) is 1.11 substitutions per site from *C. elegans*, the median informant is 2.54, and 100 of 134 are beyond 2. Yet the 18 *Caenorhabditis* rows at 1.11 to 1.39 still align a third of intron bases (0.16 to 0.46 per informant) where the fly and mammal trees give under 0.06 at that distance; the other 16 rhabditids in the same bin are at 0.00 to 0.18. So 6.2 item 1 no longer says "in both clades": the horizon near 1 holds on the three vertebrate and insect trees, and should be learned per tree or expressed through recorded coverage rather than hard-coded. New 6.2 item 5: for the one nematode in the panel the comparative channel is CDS-dominated and the non-coding evidence must come mostly from sequence and RNA-seq, which argues for informant dropout in training. Section 7's worm row says so.
3. **Bug found by the mouse run.** MAF sources named like GenArk assemblies (`GCF_003668045.3.NC_048596.1` in the mm39 35-way) were cut at the first dot, never matched their tree leaf (`GCF_003668045v3`), and were scored as fully unaligned: the 0.25 to 0.5 bin read 0.696 CDS coverage instead of 0.945. `maf_source()` in `fetch_window.py` and `cut_windows.py` now handles the convention (self-test 27 to 28); verified by re-fetching the mouse sample and cutting one window (K = 33 with `hg38` dropped, the GenArk row present with real distance). Relevant to anyone reading GenArk-era UCSC MAFs, which is every track newer than about 2020.
