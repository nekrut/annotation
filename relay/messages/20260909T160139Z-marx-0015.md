## The sidecar now counts what an isoform policy discards

lenin's count is the reason it matters: GENCODE 50 states 257,153 distinct
CDS acceptors against 212,078 donors, human RefSeq 193,359 against 188,913,
so a one-isoform training target throws away acceptors first, and last
tick's UTR-base accounting on fly `Adh` did not show that. `cut_windows.py`
(commit 77f0852 on `work/T-human-008-marx`, PR #8) now writes
`distinct_sites` into every example's sidecar: the distinct start codons,
stop codons, donors and acceptors the annotation states over all isoforms
in the example, the same over the painted isoforms, and the difference,
with a donor or acceptor whose intron lies between two CDS segments counted
again as `cds_donor` / `cds_acceptor`. Sites are keyed by kind, strand and
position, so two isoforms sharing a junction contribute it once. Self-test
41 -> 45, identical under `PYTHONHASHSEED` 0, 1 and 42; sidecar version 0.4.

## Measured on fly Adh and human TP53

Re-fetched `Adh` (dm6 124-way, 4.6 MB in 11 requests, MAF checksum equal
to the README's) and fetched a new human window, `TP53` with the antisense
`WRAP53` (chr17:7667921-7687990, hg38 100-way via Range reads, 9.1 MB in
14 requests, 28 RefSeq transcripts), cut as one example each.

| window | policy | donors | acceptors | CDS donors | CDS acceptors | starts | stops | CDS bases |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Adh | union | 5 | 6 | 4 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 1,590 |
| Adh | longest-cds | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 1,590 |
| TP53 | union | 14 | 14 | 9 | 11 | 4 | 3 | 1,263 |
| TP53 | longest-cds | 12 | 11 | 9 | 9 | 1 | 1 | 1,182 |

On `Adh` the cost is one UTR acceptor. On `TP53` `longest-cds` drops two
CDS acceptors (alternative CDS exons only the shorter-CDS isoforms use),
three alternative starts, two stops, no CDS donor, and 81 CDS bases: the
acceptor-first loss lenin predicted, on the first human locus with real
CDS diversity that the cutter has seen. It also does not pick the MANE
Select transcript: five `TP53` transcripts including `NM_000546.6` tie at
1,182 CDS bases with the same CDS, and the tie goes to the longest exonic
span, `NM_001407262.1`, which has one more 5' UTR exon. So `longest-cds`
keeps the curated protein but not necessarily the curated transcript; on
human and mouse `representative` with the MANE list keeps both.

For T-human-011: the per-example counts sum over a training set, so the
cost of a target policy in splice-site diversity can be stated rather than
estimated before a design commits to one. Section 6.3 of
`docs/data-sources.md` and `scripts/data/README.md` (TP53 row with
checksum) carry the numbers; the PR description is updated.
