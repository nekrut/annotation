## Alternative candidates change the meaning of gene recovery

The paper evaluates 296 genes and 649 transcripts. A gene succeeds if any predicted transcript matches a reference transcript; transcript scoring counts individual candidates. Posterior transcript confidence means an identical start-to-stop structure; posterior gene confidence instead means same-strand coding overlap within the gene boundaries. Alternatives come from sampled parses plus the Viterbi parse, followed by filtering. [Stanke et al. (2006), DOI 10.1093/nar/gkl200, Methods and Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1538822/).

## Posterior ranking does not establish calibration against annotation

In the single-transcript run, 545/612 exons with posterior above 99% exactly match annotation: 89.05% by my arithmetic. Across bins, 1,456/2,277 match. [Table 1 and its explanatory footnote](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1538822/#tbl1). The highest-bin posterior therefore exceeds observed correctness by more than 9.9477 percentage points. This comparison is against the paper's reference; it does not establish that every unmatched prediction is biologically false. It strengthens the case for measuring calibration against a specified reference separately from ranking quality.

The companion EGASP article reports different ab initio gene Sn/Sp, 24.3%/17.2%, and states that alternative-transcript support was added after the workshop. It reports human training on 1,286 GenBank genes and four hours for the 21.9-Mb test regions on a 2.4-GHz processor. Keep these results distinct from the alternative-transcript table; that runtime does not establish the cost of its sampling settings. [Stanke, Tzvetkova and Morgenstern (2006), DOI 10.1186/gb-2006-7-s1-s11, Results and Materials and methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1810548/).

## Released filtering code: which confidence controls output

At the existing inventory pin `220e5a63c0fd546563472ec6d1f9271faddaf569`, `Transcript::meanStateProb` computes a geometric mean of component posterior probabilities. `filterGenePrediction` applies that mean threshold and individual coding-exon/intron thresholds when probabilities exist; `keep_viterbi` exempts Viterbi transcripts from these confidence thresholds. `filterTranscriptsByMaxTracks` prioritizes preserved Viterbi transcripts and then the mean score, limiting simultaneous interval overlap. This cap is not generally identical to a total number of transcripts per connected gene cluster. [Pinned mean calculation](https://github.com/Gaius-Augustus/Augustus/blob/220e5a63c0fd546563472ec6d1f9271faddaf569/src/gene.cc#L1241), [filters](https://github.com/Gaius-Augustus/Augustus/blob/220e5a63c0fd546563472ec6d1f9271faddaf569/src/gene.cc#L2465), [track selection](https://github.com/Gaius-Augustus/Augustus/blob/220e5a63c0fd546563472ec6d1f9271faddaf569/src/gene.cc#L2533).

The generic configuration lists `sample=100`, `alternatives-from-sampling=false`, confidence thresholds 0.08/0.4, `maxtracks=-1`, `keep_viterbi=true`, and `uniqueCDS=true`. This is a configuration-file observation, not proof of the options used for the historical human experiment. [Pinned generic parameters](https://github.com/Gaius-Augustus/Augustus/blob/220e5a63c0fd546563472ec6d1f9271faddaf569/config/species/generic/generic_parameters.cfg#L27). The active prediction path passes the thresholds to the filter. [Pinned caller](https://github.com/Gaius-Augustus/Augustus/blob/220e5a63c0fd546563472ec6d1f9271faddaf569/src/namgene.cc#L909).

My recommendations for later synthesis/design: record candidate-output limits and confidence filters beside each result; show exact-transcript precision/recall alongside any-isoform gene recovery; distinguish coding-overlap confidence, component-score summaries and exact-transcript confidence. Compare methods at declared output settings rather than selecting a favorable endpoint after seeing results. These recommendations do not modify another owner's benchmark or authorize a prototype.
