**P2 — enforce or explicitly replace the declared window-length bound.**
[`_load_all_windows`](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/ead98310a5662bd7b01c179816d78193a6d88942/model/a/train.py)
passes `max_window` to the chain loader but passes only
`background_length` to the background loader. Config validation permits
the latter to exceed the former; the optimizer loop has no later cap.
`build_manifest` still describes `max_window` as the per-window workload
bound. A source-pinned synthetic fixture with `max_window=100`,
`background_windows=1`, `background_length=1000` loads windows of
**29 and 1,000 bases**. Likewise, enabling background sampling while retaining
a smaller existing maximum can introduce longer examples through the
new default length of 4,096.

Before fitting, reject an enabled background length above a non-null
maximum, or deliberately define the old setting as a chain-only filter
and record/document the effective maximum across both kinds of window.
Both input settings already appear in the manifest; the issue is the
stated bound, not missing provenance. Add an integration check through
`_load_all_windows`, since the new config-only tests do not cover this.

**P3 — the exclusion is CDS-span-free, not free of every annotated gene
or transcript.**
[`iter_background_windows`](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/ead98310a5662bd7b01c179816d78193a6d88942/model/a/dataset.py)
uses `load_gff_rows` and `Transcript.span`.
The [admission parser](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/ead98310a5662bd7b01c179816d78193a6d88942/model/labels/admission.py)
discards entries without CDS rows, and `span` covers the CDS endpoints,
not the transcript/UTR endpoints. Thus the docstring and a-pilot claim
that all GFF3 transcripts are blocked is too broad. In the synthetic
fixture below, the first background tile is `[29,1029)`, inside the
annotated UTR; three tiles also overlap the separate ncRNA.

This occurs on the actual pinned *S. cerevisiae* inputs too. Re-running the
new loader with `count=1000000, seed=0` reproduces the reported candidate
counts. Intersecting those tiles with raw GFF3 `gene`/`pseudogene`
feature spans gives:

| window length | candidates yielded | overlap an annotated gene/pseudogene |
|---|---:|---:|
| 4,096 | 23 | 11 |
| 2,048 | 198 | 68 |
| 1,024 | 928 | 214 |

For example, `NC_001135.5:+:background:0-4096` overlaps pseudogenes
YCL075W `[2125,2566)` and YCL074W `[2823,3750)` in zero-based coordinates.
Every examined tile nevertheless avoids every parsed CDS span; this is
not evidence of incorrect protein-coding targets. Either describe this
explicitly as CDS-span-free background, which may contain UTRs and
noncoding/pseudogene annotations, or construct the broader raw-feature
exclusion the documentation currently promises. Do not use these counts
as counts of strictly intergenic windows without that distinction.
