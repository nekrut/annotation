## Dictyostelium sequence-scope reconciliation

Resolved the Ddp5 classification question identified in
[engels-0049](20260918T182557Z-engels-0049.md) by joining the pinned **train**
[assembly report](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genomes/all/GCF/000/004/695/GCF_000004695.1_dicty_2.7/GCF_000004695.1_dicty_2.7_assembly_report.txt),
[GFF](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genomes/all/GCF/000/004/695/GCF_000004695.1_dicty_2.7/GCF_000004695.1_dicty_2.7_genomic.gff.gz)
and FASTA to the committed admission manifest. All data stayed in memory.
The full reproducer below verifies source pins and prints the exact URLs.

All **41 sequences / 34,204,973 bases** agree in accession and length across
the three sources. The actual `benchmark/score.py:select_seqids` keeps
**10 sequences / 34,020,927 bases**: six chromosomes, three unplaced scaffolds,
and Ddp5. It excludes **30** sequences below 10 kb (**128,482 bases**) and the
**55,564-base** mitochondrion. The corresponding source CDS transcript
exclusions exactly reproduce the admission summary: **94** short-sequence
and **42** mitochondrial records. All **13,155** representative source
row counts, spans and CDS lengths match the manifest; its **12,937 admitted**
and **218 masked** counts agree. The **13,179** retained CDS identities
also match the accepted-transcript count. This is sequence selection and
source joining, not an independent rerun of representative selection.

Ddp5 (`NC_001889.1`, **14,955 bases**) is explicitly `Plasmid` in the
report's `Primary Assembly`. Its GFF region has `genome=plasmid`,
`Is_circular=true`, and the source note **"high copy number nuclear plasmid"**.
Thus its presence does not demonstrate an organelle leak. The GFF also
records Ddp5 as strain **WS2162**, taxon **44689**, while the chromosome
regions say **AX4**, taxon **352472**. Preserve that source metadata;
an assembly-level strain label must not overwrite sequence-level provenance.

All **six** admitted Ddp5 chains (three per strand, **eight CDS rows /
12,024 CDS bases**) independently pass spliced phase, ATG start, terminal
stop, no internal stop, and unambiguous-base checks. The reproducer prints
their coordinates and coding-sequence SHA256s. Two have introns:
`rna-d1` has CDS [5982,6059), [6129,9877), with row phases 0,1;
minus `rna-g5` has [12997,13280), [12186,12926), phases 0,2
(transcriptional order, zero-based half-open). `rna-rep` is a complete
CDS at [0,3117): coordinate zero is the circular reference origin.

**Loader consequence:** preserve the existing benchmark scope and give Ddp5
its own explicit sequence group. Requiring `genome=chromosome` would
additionally discard the three selected unplaced scaffolds (**25 admitted
targets**) as well as Ddp5; requiring location type `Chromosome` for every
selected sequence has the same problem here. Keep chromosome-development
selection separate from the full scored-sequence inventory. The six
chromosomes contain **12,906 admitted targets / 33,943,072 reference bases**.

The circular flag also means the reference origin cannot automatically be
treated as a biological sequence truncation for partial-end supervision.
This is an integration requirement inferred from the metadata and the
proposal's true-edge policy, not a demonstrated decoder bug or a new
circular decoder implementation. No current admission labels, scored
denominator, or development split changed.

no files written. Source MD5s: GFF `074ff2892412e4068538cf5df8dba350`,
