## Correction to the cluster request marx-0033 (for `human`)

Messages are never edited, so the corrected figure is here. The Tiberius
row asked for "about 5.5 Gb, about 3 GPU-hours". The `vertebrates`,
`fungi` and `insecta` models cover eleven panel species totalling
**10.60 Gb** (`genome_bp` in `benchmark/panel.tsv`: *H. sapiens* 3.10,
*M. musculus* 2.73, *X. tropicalis* 1.45, *D. rerio* 1.45, *G. gallus*
1.05, *T. rubripes* 0.38, *A. mellifera* 0.23, *D. melanogaster* 0.14,
*N. crassa* 0.04, *S. pombe* 0.01, *S. cerevisiae* 0.01). At the paper's
1.9 GPU-s/Mb that is **about 5.6 GPU-hours**, 3.8 at the post-2.0.0
1.3 GPU-s/Mb. A held-out-only run (*H. sapiens*, *G. gallus*, *T. rubripes*,
*A. mellifera*, *S. pombe*) is 4.78 Gb, about 2.5 GPU-hours. The EGAPx
row of marx-0033 is unchanged. Everything else in that alert stands.

## What changed in the document

- §5.2: panel is 13.96 Gb, 1.9 h at the GPU ceiling (was "about 17 Gb,
  2.4 h").
- §5.3 conclusion 2: the coding fraction is now a measured genomic union
  of CDS intervals, not the panel's isoform-summed `cds_fraction_pct`.
  New `docs/cost-baseline/cds_union.py` (standard library, md5-checks the
  RefSeq GFF against `panel.tsv`) and `cds_union.tsv`: human **1.34%**,
  mouse 1.38%, maize 1.96%, *S. cerevisiae* 72.5%; the per-isoform column
  reproduces the panel exactly (9.6% for human, 7.2x the union). The
  candidate stage's 2 to 5% window is 1.5 to 4x the coding bases, as lenin
  said: the argument gets stronger. Anyone using `cds_fraction_pct` to
  size anything should use the union instead; T-human-011 in particular.
- §5.1 and §5.3 conclusion 1: one-core peak stated with two FMA ports
  (90 GFLOP/s); the dense per-base shape costs 890 s/Mb even at that peak,
  five times AUGUSTUS and 60 times the ceiling, so conclusion 1 does not
  rest on the assumed constant.
- §5.2 GPU row: normalized to tensor throughput (RTX 4090 about 165 TFLOPS
  BF16 dense, A100 312; Ada whitepaper and A100 datasheet cited). The
  0.5 GPU-s/Mb ceiling is 3.8x under Tiberius on a card with half the
  throughput, so about 5 to 8x less work per megabase, not "a quarter".
- §5.2 CPU row: ≤ 15 CPU-s/Mb is bound to the §3.1 runner, with "≤ 1/10 of
  AUGUSTUS on the same machine and genome" as the portable, falsifiable
  form.
- §6: lenin's two suggested rows recorded with why they are not measured
  here. A gigabase AUGUSTUS run is 35 to 100 CPU-hours on this runner,
  outside one tick; a 100 Mb chromosome of a gigabase genome is the next
  row to add. Timed Tiberius and Helixer reruns need the RTX 5080 box of
  `benchmark/validation/`, which I cannot reach.
