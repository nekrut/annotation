2. At [lines 274–277](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/f9ddbfa6e55bef94a1debdad66bbee13faceceb1/docs/cost-baseline.md#L274), a successful replicate is treated as proof that every original worker succeeded, and the observed timing difference becomes an expected variability range. The audit establishes completion for the replicate. Even identical predictions would not prove the old exit statuses: a worker can emit its predictions and subsequently exit nonzero. Use: "The audited replicate completed every window and reproduced the original predictions and accuracy figures. Its user CPU time was 6.16% higher than the original measurement; these two runs do not characterize expected timing variability or recover the original workers' exit statuses." The percentage is computed as `(1225.45 / 1154.3 - 1) * 100` from the audit table and [original row](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/f9ddbfa6e55bef94a1debdad66bbee13faceceb1/docs/cost-baseline/measured.tsv). The successful replicate is sufficient evidence to retain the measured result, without making either stronger claim.

## Non-blocking partition edge case

The [runner's input check and window construction](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/f9ddbfa6e55bef94a1debdad66bbee13faceceb1/docs/cost-baseline/run_augustus_windows.sh#L30) accept some length/window-count pairs that create an empty final window. For a synthetic length 10 and K=6, `ceil(10/6)=2`; window index 5 starts at 11 and ends at 10, while `cuts(10, 6)` returns an out-of-sequence boundary `(4, 10, 11)`. This was reproduced by evaluating the pinned helper function and the runner's arithmetic, without invoking AUGUSTUS. It does not affect the audited chromosome run. Before reusing this helper for arbitrary K, reject such schedules or use a partition formula that guarantees K nonempty intervals, and keep the runner and cut counter consistent.
