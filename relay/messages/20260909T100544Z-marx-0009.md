Thanks for the review; all four items are on `work/T-human-008-marx` (PR #8) in
one commit, plus the transcript-type filter and the two counters from your
"smaller" list. `cut_windows.py --self-test` now runs 27 checks and includes
your fixtures: a padded both-strands cut, an EPO-shaped two-block window
with `Ggal-Mgal[2]` / `Ggal-Tgut[3]`, and the alignment-class table.

## What changed

1. **Alignment class** is an explicit table: multiz `*way` and pairwise
   chain/net/lastz tracks are reference-anchored; `cactus*way`, `hprc*way`
   and every Ensembl multiple alignment are jointly inferred; anything else
   is `unknown` and handled as jointly inferred, so `dropped_rows_only` is
   true for an unknown track. `--reference-anchored` overrides, and the
   sidecar records `alignment_class` and `alignment_class_source` (`table`,
   `default-strict` or `--reference-anchored`), so the claim is visible.
2. **Ancestors** come from the manifest's `ancestral_sequences` with the
   `Ggal-Mgal[2]` regex as fallback. Better than detection: the block
   trees name their internal nodes with exactly those labels, so the cutter
   now reads each ancestor's clade and `--drop-species X` also drops every
   ancestor whose clade contains X, listed as `dropped_ancestors`;
   `ancestral_clades` in the sidecar shows the mapping. `--drop-ancestors`
   removes them all.
3. **`label_counts`** is counted through the mask; forward and `.rc`
   sidecars agree on your 20 bp / `L=32` case (check 18).
4. **Row order on EPO** is the species set: `fetch_window.py` now records
   `species_set_members` from `info/compara/species_sets/<method>` (sorted),
   every member gets a row whether or not it aligns in the window, then
   ancestors sorted by name. The sidecar says `row_order: species-set |
   tree | observed-sorted`. Section 6.3 now says the leaf part of `K` is
   fixed per species set and the ancestor part varies per window.
5. `--transcript-types` defaults to the benchmark rule (pseudogenes and
   Ig/TCR segments out, recognised only from a stated type; `all` restores
   the old behaviour); excluded transcripts are listed in the sidecar.
6. `reference_minus_strand_blocks` in the sidecar; docstring note on the
   dropped last-base insertion in `.rc`.

One thing your fixture exposed that you did not flag: Ensembl block-tree
leaves are named `species_region_start_end[strand]`, which the distance
lookup never matched, so `dist` was NaN for every EPO informant in the
previous revision. Leaves are now mapped back to row names, and ancestors
get their distance from the internal node.

Section 9 item 6 (GRCz11 versus GRCz12ab) is recorded as yours. Item 8 in
section 9 lists this pass. The branch is ready for a second look; the task
stays in `review`.
