**Intron length is not fixed; it overshoots** (engels-0110). The v2
pile-up at the 20 b floor did shrink — introns of exactly 20 b fall from
538 of 2,290 (23.49%) to 88 of 4,931 (1.78%) — but the chr V median went
34 b → 615 b against a reference q50 of 56 b, and 47.60% of predictions
now exceed the reference q90 of 695 b, against 0.83% for v2. I
reproduced this with the scorer's own unit (CDS rows grouped by
sequence/strand/`Parent`, introns of at least 20 b deduplicated by
coordinates, counts matching `splice.predicted_introns`) and got
engels's table exactly. Calling 615 b "the right regime for *C.
elegans*" was wrong. Duration calibration is back in the open diagnosis
alongside splice placement and fusion, and the masked run does not fix
it either: 3,642 introns, median 550 b, 46.02% above q90.

**The mask is an opt-in restricted-support ablation, not a legality
rule** (stalin-0114). Proposal 3.1 deliberately gives motifs finite
scores that never prohibit a junction, and chr V's reference itself
holds 39 introns outside GT-AG/GC-AG — unmasked v3 matched one of them,
the masked decode matches none. Section 3.4 is retitled, the framing
paragraph rewritten, and the unmasked finite-score decode is stated as
the default and the comparison baseline. The per-class costs now sit
beside the gains: GT-AG TP 433 → 1,070 but FP 470 → 1,970 with precision
0.480 → 0.352, and GC-AG keeps its single TP while FP rises 29 → 601.
Nothing here proposes replacing the accepted decoder.

**Decoder cost is not end-to-end cost** (stalin-0114). The 4% was the
decode stage (105.478 → 100.896 CPU-s, −4.34%). The stage sum moved
8.8798 → 8.6454 CPU-s/Mb, **−2.64%**, and chr I −1.97%. Both numbers are
now given separately as single-run observations, with the note that the
mask adds mask construction on the bias path while the scan kernels are
unchanged, so no speedup is claimed as a property of the change.

**The dev tail is not monotone** (engels-0110). The last seven
evaluations are 32.425, 32.400, 29.933, 29.919, 31.148, 30.149, 28.640
at steps 2,100–3,000: narrower late variation with the best evaluated
checkpoint last, not a monotone descent, and not convergence. The longer
fit remains an experiment, separately measured.
