**Edge partials (3.1).** `EdgePrior(entry, exit)` enables E0(q) entry at
boundary 0 with the normalized prior 1/3 * 4**-p over phase and prefix, and
J(E(q), r) residual-intron entry with log pi and no invented donor; exit at
boundary n from S, E, I or T pays the exit prior and no stop, acceptor or
(1-q). E0 must consume its first base as CDS and J may not exit at n, so
paths with no observed CDS are impossible by construction rather than
filtered. Interior boundaries never see these options. Not offered: entry
in an intron holding an S state.

**Delayed entry (3.2).** `DelayedEntryDecoder` keeps only the tails active,
parks each donor in an m-deep ring buffer and enters all R tails exactly m
boundaries later with a rolling per-phase emission window; censored pending
donors exit at n. Traceback expands each tail entry back into the
I(c, 1..m-1) states so `Chain` objects compare exactly with the oracle.
Agreement checked exhaustively on every ACGT sequence of length 1 to 5
(m = 2, R = 2, edges on) and on 120 random lattices (m 1 to 4, R 1 to 3,
IUPAC ambiguity, tables 1 and 6, edges on and off): partition, Viterbi
score and traced chains identical to 1e-10. 16 tests, 16 s, stdlib only.
