## 2. First metazoan row on `work/T-human-009-marx` (branch only, no PR)

Commit `ac4677f` adds *C. elegans* to the draft `docs/cost-baseline.md`,
measured on this runner (4-core Xeon 2.80 GHz, 15 GB, AUGUSTUS 3.5.0 from
the Ubuntu package, one process per sequence, 4 in parallel, GNU `time -v`):

| genome | Mb | wall s | CPU-s | **CPU-s/Mb** | peak RSS | nucleotide / locus / transcript F1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *C. elegans* WBcel235 | 100.29 | 3,951 | 11,409 | **114** | 0.73 GB | 0.86887 / 0.77123 / 0.30895 |

Three findings, each written into §3.3 with its source:

- **No single panel statistic orders the per-Mb cost.** The four measured
  genomes cost 57 (*S. cerevisiae*), 90 (*P. falciparum*), 114
  (*C. elegans*) and 165 (*S. pombe*) CPU-s/Mb. *C. elegans* has 2.7x
  more introns per Mb than *S. pombe* (1,140 vs 418, `benchmark/panel.tsv`)
  and costs 1.4x less. My earlier claim that cost "tracks intron density"
  (marx-0029) was too strong; the draft now says the range is 3x and the
  budget is a ceiling over the panel. Within *C. elegans* the six nuclear
  chromosomes span 101 to 131 CPU-s/Mb, chrX dearest.
- **Memory is linear in sequence length within a species** (0.54 GB at
  14 Mb to 0.73 GB at 21 Mb) and composition-driven across species
  (0.97 GB for one 3.3 Mb *P. falciparum* chromosome).
- **The accuracy the new model has to beat on a nematode**, the clade EGAPx
  declares out of scope: with the stock `caenorhabditis` parameter set,
  14,999 predicted loci against 19,971, of which 2,874 reference loci are
  fused into a neighbour's prediction and 6,486 missed; transcript F1 0.309.
  Gene fusion in a 199-genes-per-Mb genome with 65 bp median introns is the
  failure a length-aware decoder is meant to fix, and this is the number at
  114 CPU-s/Mb.

Wall clock matters for the tick budget: 66 minutes on four cores, and the
21 Mb chrV alone held a core for 41 minutes, so anything larger than this
genome is a cluster or overnight job for AUGUSTUS on this runner. The scorer
needs no `--stop-outside-cds` for `caenorhabditis` (it sets
`stopCodonExcludedFromCDS false`, like `pfalciparum`).
