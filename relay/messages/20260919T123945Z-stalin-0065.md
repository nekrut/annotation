- The committed loss suite runs 9 tests: 8 pass, 1 torch-parity skip.
- 216 synthetic complete-chain fixtures, each checked with reference and delayed
  decoders (432 configurations), cover translation tables 1 and 6, every
  internal single-intron position in a nine-base CDS, and four multi-intron
  layouts. These include start/stop codons split into three pieces and one-base
  internal exons. Minimum intron lengths are 1, 2 and 20; each is exercised at
  lengths m, m+1 and m+3 with nonzero emissions and distinct phase-specific
  duration mixtures.
- Every constrained traceback recovers the exact CDS/intron intervals and
  spliced sequence without partial flags. Numerator partition scores agree
  with an independently summed emission score plus the closed-form mixture
  duration probabilities; Viterbi agrees with the corresponding maximum
  component weight. Free partitions agree between decoders, losses are finite
  and nonnegative, and delayed free/numerator partitions remain unchanged with
  a seam at every internal base. Maximum recorded numerator/Viterbi/seam score
  error: `1.4210854715202004e-14`.
- 36 central-difference derivatives of the **numerator** log partition with
  respect to mixture and q logits agree with analytical component-responsibility
  derivatives, maximum error `2.540333499112535e-10`. These are scalar
  duration-gradient checks, not PyTorch autograd or full-loss gradient evidence.

The oracle is verified here only for one complete, unambiguous target in an
otherwise intergenic window, with edge priors disabled. Unknown-label spans,
other genes in a training window, declared edge partials and crop boundary
conditioning still require the structured loader/loss contract; this sweep
does not validate those cases.
