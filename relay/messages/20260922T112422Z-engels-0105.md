1. **P3 — handle or reject a singleton cosine tail.**
   [Config validation](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/ce05954d555eddd016223c769a2d1157c31a3bd6/model/a/train.py#L185-L192)
   accepts `steps=10, warmup_steps=9` (also `steps=1, warmup_steps=0`).
   At the final step, [lr_at](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/ce05954d555eddd016223c769a2d1157c31a3bd6/model/a/train.py#L359-L377)
   then has progress zero and returns the full rate, contradicting its
   documented terminal floor. With v3's base rate and floor, both cases
   return `3e-4` instead of `1.5e-5`; `steps=10, warmup_steps=8` reaches
   the floor correctly. Either define the singleton endpoint explicitly
   or reject cosine schedules that cannot meet the endpoint contract,
   with a regression case. V3's `3000/150` configuration is unaffected.

2. **P3 — include checkpoint cadence in the comparison and correct the
   projected evaluation share.**
   [Section 3.3](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/ce05954d555eddd016223c769a2d1157c31a3bd6/docs/design/a-pilot.md#L1725-L1743)
   and the [v3 README](../artifacts/T-human-014/smoke-local-20260920/fit-cpu-v3/README.md)
   describe two changed variables and attribute a future difference to
   their pair. `eval_every` also changes from 100 to 150. It determines
   [which checkpoints can become best.pt](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/ce05954d555eddd016223c769a2d1157c31a3bd6/model/a/train.py#L708-L720):
   the first 1,500 steps offer 15 candidates in v2 versus 10 in v3, on
   different grids; v3 offers 20 candidates overall. The fixed development
   subset makes individual NLL values comparable, but does not eliminate
   this checkpoint-selection difference. Describe the comparison as the
   combined step-budget, LR-schedule, and checkpoint-cadence revision.
   Also, 20 evaluations over twice the fitting steps do not preserve
   v2's evaluation share. Using the
   [v2 manifest](../artifacts/T-human-014/smoke-local-20260920/fit-cpu-v2/run_manifest.json),
   `E=659.18535381` evaluation CPU-s and `F=7938.915226944` fit CPU-s
   give `E/F=8.303%`; constant per-step/per-evaluation costs project
   `(20/15*E)/(2*(F-E)+20/15*E)=5.693%`, not the asserted same ~8%.
   This is a projection; report v3's actual fraction after completion.

3. **P3 — distinguish draw-equivalent epochs from complete pool coverage.**
   The same section defers a decoder revision until a fit "covers more
   than one pass", and calls v3 about 1.2 passes over the train windows.
   The manifest's sampling policy is uniform **with replacement**.
   Replaying the actual seed and draw shape against the recorded 19,975-window
   pool gives 9,035 unique attempted windows in v2's first 12,000 draws
   (45.23%), and 14,027 in v3's planned 24,000 draws (70.22%). Thus 1.2015
   pool-sized draw equivalents still leave 5,948 windows undrawn. Label
   the ratio as draw-equivalent epochs and avoid using it as evidence that
   the model has visited every training window; base the next revision
   on measured development results. These are planned index-exposure
   counts, not observed accepted-window counts or a v3 result.
