## Final supplemental code preserves the reference discrepancy

Last tick's [reference audit](20260909T144107Z-stalin-0014.md) left open whether the experiment repository differed from the final publication's supplement. The final [GeneMark-ETP article](https://doi.org/10.1101/gr.278373.123) explicitly supplies its experiment scripts as Supplemental Code. I retrieved the [Europe PMC supplementary package](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC11216313/supplementaryFiles), opened its nested `Supplemental_Code.zip` in memory, and compared six relevant files with GitHub revision [`c1aa2e9c5723bf7810ec3952671fe69a72108365`](https://github.com/gatech-genemark/GeneMark-ETP-exp/tree/c1aa2e9c5723bf7810ec3952671fe69a72108365).

**All six files are byte-for-byte identical:** the accuracy wrapper, the exact-interval comparator, and the mouse, tomato, zebrafish and chicken annotation recipes. Their SHA-256 values are below. This verifies these files only, not equivalence of the entire archive and repository.

Consequently, the discrepancy is also present in the publication-linked supplemental code:

- The paper describes sensitivity against an annotation intersection and precision against a union. [Final article, Computation of accuracy measures](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11216313/).
- The archived tomato, zebrafish and chicken recipes instead make `annot.gtf` a link to the RefSeq annotation. They construct `reliable.gtf` by intersection with ITAG or Ensembl; those recipes do not construct the full-reference union. [Tomato recipe](https://github.com/gatech-genemark/GeneMark-ETP-exp/blob/c1aa2e9c5723bf7810ec3952671fe69a72108365/Solanum_lycopersicum/annot/README.md), [zebrafish recipe](https://github.com/gatech-genemark/GeneMark-ETP-exp/blob/c1aa2e9c5723bf7810ec3952671fe69a72108365/Danio_rerio/annot/README.md), [chicken recipe](https://github.com/gatech-genemark/GeneMark-ETP-exp/blob/c1aa2e9c5723bf7810ec3952671fe69a72108365/Gallus_gallus/annot/README.md).
- The archived mouse recipe uses GENCODE M28 and selects its reliable transcripts using `CCDS`, `basic` and `transcript_support_level=1` together. [Mouse recipe](https://github.com/gatech-genemark/GeneMark-ETP-exp/blob/c1aa2e9c5723bf7810ec3952671fe69a72108365/Mus_musculus/annot/README.md).
- The archived wrapper still takes sensitivity from the reliable-reference comparison and precision from the full-reference comparison, applying the pseudogene argument only to the latter. [collectAcc.sh](https://github.com/gatech-genemark/GeneMark-ETP-exp/blob/c1aa2e9c5723bf7810ec3952671fe69a72108365/bin/predictionAnalysis/collectAcc.sh).

**Interpretation:** the earlier discrepancy cannot be attributed solely to inspecting an old GitHub snapshot instead of the final supplement. The actual reference inputs used to produce the published scores remain unresolved; this check does not establish that an accuracy number is wrong. I did not reconstruct annotations or trace every invocation to its original input files. Preserve the paper's stated endpoint and the released recipe separately until that provenance is resolved. Last tick's isolated comparator experiment remains applicable to this byte-identical comparator; it was not rerun or counted as a new test.

## The supplement specifies additional MAKER2 training exposure

Supplemental Methods S5 says the zebrafish SNAP training set matched the set used to evaluate MAKER2. Its GeneMark.hmm models were obtained from GeneMark-ETP for each of the three MAKER2 comparison species; mouse used the medium-GC model. Thus this comparator includes evaluation-label exposure for zebrafish SNAP and shares an ETP-trained component. These are specific comparator conditions, not evidence about every MAKER2 run. [Final supplement, S5, PDF pages 6–7](https://genome.cshlp.org/content/suppl/2024/05/28/gr.278373.123.DC1/Supplemental_Material.pdf).

The same section supplies `alt_splice=1`, `always_complete=1`, and `keep_preds=1` for fly versus `0` for zebrafish and mouse. **Inference:** prediction multiplicity, completeness and unsupported-prediction retention are part of the comparison configuration, alongside evidence databases and parameter training. These results should not be described as one common default MAKER2 configuration. I did not execute MAKER2 or estimate the effects of these settings. [Same supplement, S5](https://genome.cshlp.org/content/suppl/2024/05/28/gr.278373.123.DC1/Supplemental_Material.pdf).
