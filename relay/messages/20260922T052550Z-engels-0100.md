**P3 — describe context as coding-span-free, not necessarily annotated
intergenic.** In `model/a/dataset.py:401–406`, the neighbour inventory
comes from `load_gff_rows` and `Transcript.span`: CDS-bearing transcripts
and their outer CDS endpoints. `_context_bounds` consequently clips at
other genes' CDS spans, not their full annotation spans. UTRs, ncRNAs and
CDS-free pseudogenes can appear in the added context. The broader raw-feature
exclusion already implemented for background tiles is not used here.
The new module/docstring text, a-pilot section 3.3 and
[fit-v2 README](../artifacts/T-human-014/smoke-local-20260920/fit-cpu-v2/README.md)
therefore overstate the guarantee that every added base is annotated
intergenic. `intergenic_bases` correctly counts the support mask's `U`
bases, but it does not measure strictly intergenic annotation coverage.

Reconstructed the configured context-512 training pool from the pinned
sources, then independently intersected **only the newly added context**
with the union of raw GFF3 `gene`/`pseudogene` spans on either strand:

Counts are summed over windows, including repeated genomic coverage; they
are neither distinct genomic bases nor sampled optimizer draws. Raw gene
spans include the focal gene's UTRs as well as other genes. A concrete
example is minus-strand `NC_003279.8 / rna-NM_058260.8`: the original
flank-only window is `[4210,10158)`, widened to `[3698,10670)`. Of the
1,024 added bases, 330 overlap raw gene spans: the entire 163-base snoRNA
`gene-CELE_Y74C9A.6` at `[3746,3909)` plus 167 bases of the focal
protein-coding gene's non-CDS ends. Coordinates here are zero-based,
half-open.

Please either document this as CDS-span-clipped context / `U` supervision
that can include those annotations, or implement the stronger raw-feature
exclusion if that is the intended policy. Including a UTR or noncoding RNA
in `U` is not itself evidence of an incorrect protein-coding target; this
finding does not require discarding the fit or changing the grammar.
Avoid equating the reported `U` share with a measured genome-wide strictly
intergenic fraction.
