## What this tick added

- **Non-coding alignability as a statistic, not an anecdote** (section 6.1
  of `docs/data-sources.md`, `scripts/data/coverage_by_distance.py`). A
  fixed-seed sample of 12 fly genes (dm6 multiz124way, chr2L) and 10 human
  genes (hg38 multiz100way, chr11; short genes, 3 to 12 kb) fetched with
  `fetch_window.py`, coverage per informant per annotation class binned by
  patristic distance on the track tree. Base-weighted coverage of introns
  and intergenic sequence: below 0.5 substitutions per site 0.56 to 0.94;
  between 0.5 and 1, a quarter to three quarters of pairs; past 1, under
  0.06 with 1 to 3% of pairs above half. CDS persists: 0.73 (fly) and 0.48
  (human) beyond 2 substitutions per site. Both clades give the same shape.
  110 of the fly 124-way's 123 informants and 34 of the human 100-way's 99
  sit past that horizon, so the informant rows that carry an intron or UTR
  signal are about 13 of 123 for fly and 65 of 99 for human.
- **Window-cutting convention** (section 6.3, `scripts/data/cut_windows.py`,
  stdlib only, writes `.npz` by hand): fixed reference length with stride,
  per-base labels with CDS frame, strand and boundary marks, one row per
  tree leaf with explicit `gap` versus `unaligned` states, an
  insertion-length channel so examples keep fixed length, tree distances,
  conservation, `--drop-species` for the leakage rule, `--both-strands`.
  Three design choices are left to T-human-011: fixed versus variable
  informant axis, codon position as input or label, 5'/3' UTR split.
- Ensembl licence re-read and quoted (no restrictions on data; third-party
  constraints clause). `hgdownload.soe.ucsc.edu` reset every connection for
  several minutes mid-run while `hgdownload2` served the same files; the
  fetcher now rotates mirrors. A pipeline reading MAF by Range at scale
  should rsync per-chromosome MAF once instead.
