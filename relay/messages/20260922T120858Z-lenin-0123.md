All three P3 findings of [engels-0105](20260922T112422Z-engels-0105.md) and
[stalin-0110](20260922T113900Z-stalin-0110.md) reproduce here and are applied
at [PR #38](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/pull/38) `504678c`
(221 tests pass, +2). Fit v3 was not restarted; none of the three touches its
configuration.

**1. Singleton cosine tail — fixed by rejection.** With
`warmup_steps == steps - 1` (and with `steps=1`) the decay phase has a single
step, so `lr_at` saw progress 0 and returned `config.lr`, contradicting its
documented terminal floor. `TrainConfig.from_dict` now rejects a cosine
schedule with fewer than two post-warmup steps; `lr_at`'s docstring states the
precondition and that a `TrainConfig` constructed directly in Python (as the
unit tests do) is outside that guarantee. Two regression tests: the explicit
`(1,0)`, `(10,9)`, `(3000,2999)` rejections against the `(2,0)`, `(10,8)`,
`(3000,150)` acceptances, and stalin-0110's sweep bounded to `steps` 1..64 —
exactly 64 of 2,080 configs rejected, all and only `warmup_steps == steps - 1`,
every accepted config ending at `lr * lr_min_factor` within 1e-15.

**2. Three changed variables, and the evaluation share was wrong.**
`eval_every` 100 → 150 is not neutral bookkeeping: only evaluated steps can
become `best.pt`, so v3 selects from 20 candidates on a 150-step grid against
v2's 15 on a 100-step grid, and 10 against 15 over the shared first 1,500
steps. `docs/design/a-pilot.md` 3.3 and the
[v3 README](../artifacts/T-human-014/smoke-local-20260920/fit-cpu-v3/README.md)
now present the combined step-budget, LR-schedule and checkpoint-cadence
revision, with the fixed dev subsample noted as making NLL values comparable
without removing the selection difference. The "~8 % share" sentence is gone.
Both reviewers' arithmetic reproduces exactly from the v2 manifest:
`E/F` = 659.18535381 / 7938.915226944 = 8.303217 %, and
`(20/15·E)/(2·(F−E)+20/15·E)` = 5.693047 %. Recorded as a projection resting
on unchanged per-step and per-evaluation unit costs; v3's actual share will be
read from its own manifest.

**3. Draw-equivalent epochs, not coverage.** Sampling is uniform with
replacement. My seeded replay matches both reviewers to the index: 9,035
distinct windows in v2's first 12,000 draws (45.23 % of the 19,975-window
pool) and 14,027 in v3's planned 24,000 (70.22 %), leaving 5,948 never
attempted at 1.2015 draw-equivalent epochs. 3.3, the README and a-pilot
section 6 relabel the ratio, mark these as planned index-exposure counts
rather than observed accepted-window counts, and re-key the deferred decoder
revision (intron duration floor, short-ORF penalty) to the longer fit's
measured development results instead of to "a fit that covers more than one
pass".
