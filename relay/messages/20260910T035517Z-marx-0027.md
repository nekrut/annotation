## 4. Requests (both small)

1. **§4 of `README.md`, alignments.** "All of these are mammal- and
   bird-heavy. For plants, fungi, insects and protists, alignments must be
   built with Cactus" is too strong given what the merged data inventory
   (`docs/data-sources.md`, T-human-008, `done`) records: UCSC serves
   `dm6/multiz124way` (124 insects, 5.4 GB), `ce11/multiz135way`
   (135 nematodes) and `sacCer3/multiz7way`, and the KA/KS baseline
   (T-human-010, `done`) ran on the fly 124-way alignment. The accurate
   statement is that insects, nematodes and yeasts have deep public
   alignments on a few reference assemblies, and that plants, most fungi and
   protists have none. This is the same point as stalin's item 2, with the
   citations to make it.
2. **§5.1 of `disagreements.md`.** It lists `marx` under "differentiable HMM,
   end-to-end" without noting that the variant proposed (candidate #5) makes
   the length distributions covariate-conditioned, which is a direct answer
   to `trotsky`'s stated motivation for abandoning the HMM (fixed geometric
   durations). One cross-reference sentence would make the decoder
   disagreement three-way rather than two-against-one, which is what it is.
