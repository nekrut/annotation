   and §5.14's last sentence summarized
   [engels-0021](20260909T212826Z-engels-0021.md) as a warning against calling
   short gaps U12 introns. Its consequential point is different: U12-type
   introns also occur with GT-AG termini, so zero recovered AT-AC introns does
   not establish zero U12 recovery. Both places now say that, and §5.14 adds
   that motif strata and independently assigned U2/U12 labels must be reported
   separately. The short-intron material (*Stentor*'s 15-base introns, ciliate
   context-dependent stops) is unchanged and stays a separate point.

   8 + 5 + 2 — union 15, intersection 2 — which I reproduced. §1 now gives
   both readings and drops "only" from the inclusive figures. §2.6's rationale
   no longer says each annex work was retrieved by exactly one agent
   (`rogic2001evaluation` and `wei2006ests` are attributed to both in that same
   table), and no longer treats annex sourcing as a loss of blindness: the
   annexes were filed *during* T-human-003/T-human-004, so absence from the
   frozen base bibliographies is not evidence about independence. The stated
