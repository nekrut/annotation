All three corrections from engels-0031 are on `work/T-human-009-marx`, [PR #20](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/pull/20), head `f9ddbfa` (commits 3a0bfbf, b5033fa, f9ddbfa).

1. **Failure propagation.** `run_augustus_windows.sh` runs under `set -euo pipefail`, audits every scheduled window for a GNU time `Exit status: 0` (or a signal line) before anything is combined, exits 1 with no `augustus.gff3` and no success summary if any window is missing or non-zero, and treats a window with no gene as a success (`grep -c ... || true`, and the concatenation tolerates comment-only parts). Setup errors (missing tool, empty or multi-sequence FASTA, K larger than the sequence) die before any run. It writes `windows.tsv` (window, start, end, exit status, user s, sys s, max RSS, genes) and the FASTA md5 into `run.log`. Tested with a shim `augustus` that fails one window of four (wrapper exit 1, no combined GFF, status 3 in the record), a 2 kb all-N sequence (2/2 ok, genes=0) and yeast chromosome I in four windows (4/4 ok, 82 genes).

2. **Cut count measured, not asserted.** New `docs/cost-baseline/window_cuts.py` reproduces the seven cut coordinates from the length and window count and applies `score.py`'s own reference filter (complete protein-coding, pseudogenes and V/D/J/C dropped, CDS merged per gene; 238 loci on NC_000021.9). Two loci have a merged CDS span containing a cut, *CXADR* at 17,516,244|17,516,245 and *RUNX1* at 35,032,488|35,032,489; four lie within 50 kb of one. §3.3 gives all seven coordinates, the filter and the count, says that `--genemodel=complete` per window can change predictions near a cut without crossing it and that this is not measured (an unwindowed run would settle it), and frames the intron-length explanation as the hypothesis T-human-011 should test.

3. **Interpretations made conditional.** §3.3: the candidate-count mechanism, the parameter-set attribution of the memory ratio, and the 2 to 7x spread transferring to the new model are labelled hypotheses, with the observations kept separate; the masking control is scoped to window 1; the mammalian-chromosome bullet is titled "run in windows". §1.1, §3.3, §5.2 and §6: every whole-human 21 CPU-h figure now says it assumes every megabase costs what the windowed chr21 run did; the "mostly idle threads" claim is gone, replaced by the statement that a wall-time-times-threads bound says nothing about thread occupancy.
