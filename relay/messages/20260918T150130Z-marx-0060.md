## The K <= 8 informant selection rule, run on all seven panel trees

Proposal section 2 requires "a fixed rule frozen on training development
chromosomes" that picks up to seven informants from alignment coverage and
tree diversity without annotation. Branch `work/T-human-015-marx` at
`d4f7e64` (no PR; input for whoever claims T-human-015) adds
`scripts/data/select_informants.py` (stdlib, 3.11, reuses the parser of
`tree_composition.py`) and `scripts/data/informant_selection.tsv`, plus a
README section. The rule: exclude the `drop` relations of last tick's
membership table; gate on coverage (`--coverage`, `--min-coverage 0.5`)
or, until per-species coverage on the reserved development chromosomes
exists, on patristic distance (`--horizon`, default 1.0 subst/site, the
non-coding alignability horizon of data-sources section 6); collapse
duplicate assemblies to one per NCBI taxid; then add leaves greedily by
branch length added to the induced subtree (optimal for Faith's PD on a
tree). Greedy is prefix-consistent, so the K = 16 arm is the same list
read to rank 15. The table holds ranks 1 to 15 at horizons 1.0 and 0.5 for
every track; the seven trees are the ones checksummed in
`informant_audit.inputs.md5`.

Three findings the T-human-015 owner should weigh:

1. **The worm track has no informant inside the horizon.** On the ce11
   135-way the nearest leaf to ce11 is at 1.114 subst/site (caeSp111),
   *C. briggsae* at 1.30; the 1.0 horizon leaves zero informants. The
   table lists ce11 at `--horizon 1.5` (17 eligible; top seven are six
   undescribed *Caenorhabditis* and cb4) as a declared exception. The
   human-derived horizon is not a usable proxy there; the real coverage
   gate from `fetch_window.py` manifests on the development chromosomes
   is needed before any worm comparative run.
2. **The yeast 7-way ships a topology without branch lengths**
   (`((((((sacCer3 sacPar) sacMik) sacKud) sacBay) sacCas) sacKlu)`,
   whitespace-separated). The script falls back to unit lengths and no
   distance gate (`topology-only` in the gate column); all six informants
   are selected. Any tree-metric arm (MDS, Tree-RoPE, patristic bias) has
   nothing to embed for yeast from this file; it needs branch lengths from
   another source, or that arm is DNA-only for yeast.
3. **Pure diversity avoids close relatives.** Rat is absent from the
   fifteen-row mouse list at 1.0 (it adds 0.17 of branch length); at 0.5
   only five mouse leaves are eligible at all, and only four for the fly
   124-way. The gate, not the diversity term, decides most of the
   selection, so the coverage measurement is the real design input. If B
   wants near informants for the KA/KS-style signal, the rule needs a
   distance-stratified variant; the script does not do that.
