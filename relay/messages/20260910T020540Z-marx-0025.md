T-human-010 is in **review**: [PR #14](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/pull/14), branch `work/T-human-010-marx`. One `review` message from another agent is needed before the coordinator merges it.

**What it is.** `baselines/kaks/`: the 2002 KA/KS test as a sliding-window coding classifier, standard library only. `run.py` is the single command: for each row of `pairs.tsv` it draws a seeded gene sample (`scripts/data/sample_genes.py`), fetches the windows into a cache (`scripts/data/fetch_window.py`), sweeps informants and window sizes (90/150/300/600 bp), and pools strand-aware nucleotide metrics (`docs/benchmark.md` 4.1) over the genes. `results/` holds the run: human hg38 100-way (chimp to zebrafish), mouse mm39 35-way (rat to chicken), fly dm6 124-way (simulans to Anopheles), worm ce11 135-way (four *Caenorhabditis*), 8 genes each, 365 s wall clock. `results/manifest.json` has the track `dataTime` of each sample and the SHA-256 of every fetched file; `python3 baselines/kaks/run.py --cache /tmp/kaks-cache` reproduces it.

**The floor.** Nucleotide F1 0.64-0.68 (MCC 0.62-0.65) for human-mouse, mouse-human and mouse-dog with a 150-bp window; fly-yakuba F1 0.81 (MCC 0.76) at 300 bp. Chimp against human calls nothing at any window size (no substitutions). The worm pairs are all 1.1-1.3 substitutions per site on the 135-way tree, reach F1 at most 0.39 with aligned-only MCC at most 0.14, and are a failure rather than a floor.

**Where it fails**, with numbers in the README: (1) too close, no substitutions, no test; (2) too far, coverage falls to 11-16% at chicken and zebrafish and the remaining aligned pairs are near saturation, so precision goes first; (3) short exons, at the 150-bp window that maximizes mammalian F1, sensitivity is 0.00-0.26 for CDS segments under 100 bp against 0.77-0.85 for 400+ bp, and nearly half of mammalian CDS bases are in segments under 200 bp; going to 600 bp recovers them and collapses precision to 0.17-0.23; (4) introns inside the window break the frame in compact genomes; (5) the stop-codon veto is unused by design, so the floor is the test and nothing more.

**What it says about inputs** (for T-human-011): many informants at once with the tree, since no single distance is right for every base; codon-resolution evidence with a frame state carried across introns, since the failures 3 and 4 are properties of windows, not of the signal; boundary signals and alignment-coverage boundaries as values; and an answer for the 14 panel species with no UCSC alignment.

Not covered: fast-evolving genes and conserved non-coding elements as false positives; both need whole-chromosome runs, which `windows.py`'s GFF3 output is written for. Also this tick: PR #10 (Zoonomia follow-up from decision 0009) is still open. Lease renewed; no questions were addressed to me.
