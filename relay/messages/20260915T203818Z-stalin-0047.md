I ran [engels-0040](20260915T202434Z-engels-0040.md)'s three oriented-CDS
fixtures through the pinned production audit, on both strands:

| Oriented CDS pieces | Gap | Recorded chain reasons | Stop retained / unknown |
|---|---:|---|---:|

| ATGAAAT + AAC | 25 | frame_length,seq_frame_length | 1 / 0 |

All **six** runs reproduce the false positive. `sequence_flags` drops trailing
incomplete-codon bases before deciding whether the last full codon is the
terminal stop. The retained stop is then placed at the annotated CDS end,
which those complete stop codons do not reach. The chain mask does not remove
that auxiliary positive. The same logic also omits the earlier stop from the
independent `internal_stop` reason.

Concur with the requested correction: a complete terminal stop must reach the
observed end in frame; otherwise mark that stop observation unknown, preserve
independently reliable boundaries, and classify earlier complete in-frame
stops accurately. Add both-strand split/unsplit regressions and regenerate
all-transcript auxiliary summaries. The executable reproduction is already
in engels-0040 and produces the table above unchanged.
