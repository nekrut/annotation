**P2 — qualify the selection-bias conclusion before fitting.**
[a-pilot.md lines 300–309](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/512564182974ef99f227c73b19adcd793eb4a6d3/docs/design/a-pilot.md#L300)
ends by asserting that no measured fraction is low enough to bias the pilot.
The table measures retention, with no declared acceptance threshold or
comparison of retained versus excluded target characteristics. The loader
excludes windows specifically because of neighboring gene overlap, so this is
selection on locus architecture, not random loss. High overall retention can
coexist with systematic removal of a smaller gene stratum or an uneven effect
on the eventual development chromosomes. Neither absence nor magnitude of bias
has been measured here. Keep the useful retention counts, replace that sentence
with an explicit unassessed-bias limitation, and treat the proposed connection
to gene density as a hypothesis unless density/spacing is actually analyzed.
Before making a no-bias claim, compare retained/excluded targets by relevant
locus, length/intron and chromosome strata under the final training policy.
This finding does not establish that the current pilot is biased; it identifies
an unsupported conclusion in a measurement deliverable.

**Clarify what the zero length-skip column measures.** The
[CLI default](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/512564182974ef99f227c73b19adcd793eb4a6d3/model/a/coverage.py#L186)
and both loader/report defaults are `max_window=None`, and the
[length filter](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/512564182974ef99f227c73b19adcd793eb4a6d3/model/a/dataset.py)
only increments its counter when a finite limit is supplied. Thus zero is
expected by construction: these rows measure unrestricted whole-gene loading.
They do not measure fit within an encoder core or the cost of the pending crop
implementation. Please spell out `max_window=None` instead of “default window.”
The document already lists crop support as pending; preserve that distinction.

The last column is the conservative bound `max(0, long_admitted - all_excluded)`:
even if every exclusion removed a long gene, that many must remain, conditional
on the reported yielded counts being correct. It is not an exact classification

[manifests](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/tree/9730c25/model/labels/manifests)
and the [reported table](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/512564182974ef99f227c73b19adcd793eb4a6d3/docs/design/a-pilot.md#L311).
This reinforces the existing crop requirement without imposing a gene-length cap.
