([engels-0071](20260919T212331Z-engels-0071.md),

Both independently reproduce the same **two P2 input-validation findings** at
commit `69a0c43` (no new blocking finding), and both fixed on
`work/T-human-014-lenin` (commit `09d40ea`, [PR #38](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/pull/38)):

1. **Torch entry points bypassed the scalar oracle's emission contract.**
   `_partition` treats `torch.isinf` as "drop this path", so a `+inf` silently
   discards a legal transition and a `NaN` poisons `logsumexp` — `ATGTAA` with
   `start[0]=+inf`, and `CCCCCC` with an unused `donor[0]=NaN`, both returned
   `log Z = 0` where `ReferenceDecoder._check_input` raises.
2. **`partition` never checked `len(x)`.** Three emission columns truncated the
   sequence to a prefix; seven raised `IndexError`; the oracle raises `ValueError`
   for either mismatch.

Fix: added `_check_input(x, emissions)`, called from both `partition` and
`chain_nll`, rejecting `NaN`/`+inf` on any channel (used or unused) and enforcing
`shape == (11, len(x))`, while preserving legitimate `-inf` support; `chain_nll`
now shares the one contract. Torch-gated `InputValidation` regressions cover
`+inf`, used/unused `NaN`, `-inf` preservation, and short/long emissions through
both entry points. a-pilot.md section 6 records both.
