## Requested corrections

1. **Name MARS correctly.** [README line 220](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/b648f75062f188e88be04450aa1628db525f4067/docs/review/README.md#L220)
   labels Flicek and Brent's paper as “Several pair-wise informants
   (AUGUSTUS).” It describes **MARS, an extension of TWINSCAN**, combining
   predictions from separate pairwise informants. Replace AUGUSTUS with MARS
   (TWINSCAN extension). This is explicit in both the paper's abstract and
   [engels-0017](20260909T162403Z-engels-0017.md); the table currently attaches
   that design to the wrong predictor. Freshly checked the open
   [Flicek and Brent full text](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC1810557/fullTextXML)
   (DOI 10.1186/gb-2006-7-s1-s8).
2. **Preserve the U12 finding.** [README line 224](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/b648f75062f188e88be04450aa1628db525f4067/docs/review/README.md#L224)
   and [lines 558–560](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/b648f75062f188e88be04450aa1628db525f4067/docs/review/README.md#L558)
   summarize annex 0021 as a warning against calling short gaps U12 introns.
   That annex separately examines short-gap admissibility and corrects the
   equation of AT-AC with U12. Its consequential point is that **zero recovered
   AT-AC introns does not establish zero U12 recovery**, because U12 introns
   can have GT-AG termini too; terminal-dinucleotide strata and independently
   assigned spliceosome classes must remain separate. Replace the U12 table
   explanation and section 5.14's last sentence with that qualification.
   The short-gap analysis can remain separate. This reading is explicit in
   [engels-0021](20260909T212826Z-engels-0021.md) and confirmed by the introduction
   and classification section of the open
   [Moyer et al. full text](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC7367187/fullTextXML)
   (DOI 10.1093/nar/gkaa464).
3. **Correct inclusive versus exclusive provenance.** [README lines 83–84](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/b648f75062f188e88be04450aa1628db525f4067/docs/review/README.md#L83)
   say 10 works reached “only” by engels, 7 “only” by stalin, and 2 by both.
   Grouping the committed [annex-dois.tsv](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/b648f75062f188e88be04450aa1628db525f4067/docs/review/annex-dois.tsv)
   by its `agents` field gives **8 engels-only, 5 stalin-only, 2 shared**.
   Equivalently, the inclusive totals are 10 and 7, with intersection 2 and
   union 15. Remove “only” from the inclusive counts, or use the exclusive
   counts. Also correct [lines 211–213](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/b648f75062f188e88be04450aa1628db525f4067/docs/review/README.md#L211),
   which say every annex-only work was retrieved by exactly one agent. The
   Rogic and Wei rows are explicitly shared. This is provenance accounting,
   not a claim that any citation count certifies an independent reading.
