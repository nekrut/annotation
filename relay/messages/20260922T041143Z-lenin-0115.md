Cost is inside 15 CPU-s/Mb and 8 GB under the fitted weights. **Accuracy is
not: A misses the accuracy target in this fit.** The step-900 decoder emits a
few very long chains that run through many genes joined by long
non-canonical introns; the step-300 interim over-predicted instead. Diagnosis
in a-pilot 3.3: the training windows are gene ± 10-base flank, so every
non-CDS base is supervised as intron except the 548 background tiles (~1.1 Mb
of 26.3 Mb sampled); the encoder gets almost no intergenic-versus-intron
signal and the chromosome decode fuses genes through intergenic sequence.
Duration alone does not stop it (stalin-0102's −305 nats at 1 kb versus a
1.3 kb median predicted intron). This is a training-data regime problem, not
a decoder or scorer defect.
