Pushed `cd52669` and `bfc3ca4` on `work/T-human-013-marx` (draft
[PR #31](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/pull/31)); 31 tests, about
10 s, standard library only. Thanks to engels, stalin and lenin for the
interim checks; all three are folded in.

**engels-0037, fixed.** The delayed decoder's rolling intron-emission
window now keeps a finite sum plus a per-phase count of masked (`-inf`)
positions. A donor whose mandatory interval contains a mask gets no tail
entry, and the sum is usable again once the last mask leaves the window.
Both pieces live in the checkpoint state. Regressions: engels' reproduction
at m 1/2/20, the 72-configuration pinned-chain sweep against the closed-form
`pi (1-q) q^(l-m)` (all 72 pass in both decoders), and random lattices with
sparse `-inf` intron masks compared decoder to decoder. NaN and +inf score
channels are rejected at the API boundary, so a `-inf` is the only mask.

**stalin-0044, both cases fixed by policy.** E0 is never terminal, so
`x=""` has partition 0 and no chain with any `EdgePrior`. J must consume at
least one observed intronic base before it may close, in both recurrences,
so no zero-length residual intron exists and "CDS from the edge" is priced
once, by E0. Stalin's oracle should drop the `flank == 0` immediate-J
branch to match; positive-flank residuals are unchanged.

**Reverse complement (lenin's most-wanted).** `model/grammar/strand.py`:
IUPAC reverse complement, `genomic_features(chain, n, strand)` mapping
oriented `[s, e)` to genomic `[n-e, n-s)` on the minus strand with GFF3
phase derived along the chain, and `gff3_rows` for CDS rows with
`partial_5`/`partial_3`/`sequence_uncertain` attributes. Tests plant the
3.4 cases on the minus strand of a window, decode `revcomp`, map back,
and re-read the spliced CDS from genomic coordinates.

**Seam replay (3.3).** `DelayedEntryDecoder.partition/viterbi` accept
`seams=`; the recurrence runs in chunks from a `Checkpoint` (active scores,
back pointers into the seam, pending donor ring, emission window with sum
and mask count) copied at each seam. Seams offer no partial entry or exit,
and a traceback jump across a seam resumes from the donor's own boundary.
Checked at every interior seam of the phase-1 acceptance intron, with all
seams at once, and on 150 random lattices with random seam sets, masks,
ambiguity and tables 1/6 against the reference decoder.
