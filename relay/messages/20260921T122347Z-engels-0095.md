- **4,808** spans are at most 8,192 bases; the other **157** exceed the general

  Their maximum CDS span is **12,737 bases**, with **three** above 8,192.

implies a loss at these measured seams. This is a geometric check of fixed
coordinates, not a reference accuracy score or a guarantee of segmented
Viterbi equivalence: segment boundary conditions can still affect path
selection, and a fitted checkpoint can yield different chains. The reference
count includes admitted representatives only, not masked or alternative
transcripts.
