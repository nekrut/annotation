**P2 — distinguish the restricted-support ablation from biological
legality and the accepted grammar.**
[Proposal section 3.1](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/7875483/docs/design/proposal.md#31-shared-coordinate-system-and-coding-grammar)
explicitly says motifs add finite boundary scores and never prohibit
noncanonical junctions. The new mask deliberately changes that support;
being inference-only does not remove the difference. Yet
[a-pilot 3.4](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/7875483/docs/design/a-pilot.md#34-decoder-revision-step-1-the-hard-splice-mask-2026-09-22-no-refit-splice-placement-doubles-a-still-misses-the-target)
calls the excluded paths ones that biology does not permit and concludes
that only selection among legal sites remains. The existing reference
itself contradicts that interpretation: chr V's `other` class has 39
reference introns; unmasked v3 matches one, whereas the masked run
matches none. These are scored reference counts, not an independent
biological validation of each annotation.

Keep the switch explicitly labeled as an opt-in restricted-support
ablation, preserve the finite-score unmasked baseline, and describe the
lost reference support and per-class tradeoffs before proposing any
replacement of the accepted decoder. The net gain does not establish
that excluded junctions are biologically impossible. Even within the
retained set, GC-AG stays at one TP while FP rises 29 -> 601; GT-AG TP
rises 433 -> 1,070 while precision falls 0.47951 -> 0.35197. Both facts
belong alongside the improved donor/acceptor F1. Sources:
[unmasked score](../artifacts/T-human-014/smoke-local-20260920/fit-cpu-v3/score-final/score_NC_003283.11_seg19_ov4096_w12288_float64.json),
[masked score](../artifacts/T-human-014/smoke-local-20260920/fit-cpu-v3/score-canonical/score_NC_003283.11_seg19_ov4096_w12288_float64.json),
and [`CANONICAL_DONORS` / `CANONICAL_ACCEPTORS`](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/7875483/model/a/pooled.py).

independently grouped the committed GFF3 CDS rows by sequence, strand and

**P3 — separate total cost from decoder cost, and observation from a
repeatable speedup.** On chr V the measurement JSONs give stage-sum
CPU-s/Mb 8.8798229275 -> 8.6454211038, a **2.64%** reduction. Decode CPU
105.4780293280 -> 100.8964355460 is **4.34%** lower. The headline's
“4% less CPU” attaches the decoder percentage to the end-to-end row.
Correct that in a-pilot and `measured.tsv`, and call these single-run
observations. The mask adds bias/masking work while leaving the scan
kernels unchanged; the measurements do not establish that forbidding
paths makes computation free or guarantees a speedup. The CPU verdict
and lack of a positive B allowance remain unchanged. Sources:
[unmasked measurement](../artifacts/T-human-014/smoke-local-20260920/fit-cpu-v3/score-final/measure_NC_003283.11_seg19_ov4096_w12288_float64.json),
[masked measurement](../artifacts/T-human-014/smoke-local-20260920/fit-cpu-v3/score-canonical/measure_NC_003283.11_seg19_ov4096_w12288_float64.json).
