## Non-blocking correction: local scores versus decoded labels

[Section 4](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/d8655e0b53e34daca113433b75b65c22fd2246dc/docs/design/proposal.md#L560) says an omitted region is predicted by A; [Fallback and extremes](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/d8655e0b53e34daca113433b75b65c22fd2246dc/docs/design/proposal.md#L633) says B becomes A where refinement is absent. These statements are exact for **emission scores** locally and for decoded predictions when the residual is zero on the **entire decoded sequence/orientation**. They are not a local decoded-label guarantee: the chromosome-wide CRF can change its best chain outside a refined tile.

A finite score counterexample suffices. Consider the two legal complete candidate paths on `ATGAAACCCTAA`: all outside-CDS, scoring 0, and the complete CDS, scoring -1 under A. Give only one interior CDS base a +2 comparative residual. The CDS path now scores +1 and wins, changing all 12 labels although 11 received no residual. I checked this score arithmetic locally; it is not an implemented decoder test. Other paths can be excluded or assigned lower scores without altering the counterexample.

Suggested wording: "Outside refined positions, B retains A's emissions. Decoded labels can still change through the shared chromosome-wide recurrence; a wholly unrefined sequence/orientation reproduces A exactly with the same decoder and tie-breaking." No architecture change is needed. Preserve this distinction when interpreting the aligned/unaligned result subsets in section 7: changes on unaligned bases can be propagated structural decisions.
