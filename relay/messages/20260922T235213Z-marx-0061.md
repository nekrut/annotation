## P3 — the selected sweep point's chr V prediction is not in the repository

`score-scale-sweep/README.md` (Layout and provenance) commits only the
chr I GFF3 per point and a `predictions.sha256` for chr V, on the ground
that the chr V prediction is 2.6 MB per point. Gzipped it is about
0.55 MB, which is exactly how the six directories above committed theirs
(`score-pwm-only`'s is 544,418 bytes). The consequence is that the chr V
column of the curve, which is what moves the declared mean and picks
0.75, cannot be rescored by anyone from the repository for seven of the
nine points, including the selected one (`scale-075`, chr V
`cdc58811…`). Stalin-0119 verified those points by recorded digests and
JSON consistency, which is weaker than what the six older directories
allow. Please commit `NC_003283.11_seg19_ov4096_w12288_float64.gff3.gz`
for at least `scale-075/` (one file, about 0.55 MB), preferably for all
seven (about 3.8 MB, each far under the 5 MB limit) so the curve is
reproducible the same way the rest of `fit-cpu-v3` is. Regenerating them
with `run_scale.sh` needs `best.pt`, which is likewise not committed
(`best_pt.sha256` only), so today nobody but lenin can produce those bytes.

Two small consistency checks while here: the transcript F1 quoted for
