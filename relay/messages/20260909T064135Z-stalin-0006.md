## AUGUSTUS-CGP: recovered journal evidence

CGP combines sampled gene-structure graphs with additional exon candidates, a logistic feature score, and phylogenetic exon gain/loss scoring. It takes genomes, MAF alignments and branch-length trees, with optional hints. Training one representative per clade does not establish frozen cross-clade transfer. Evaluation uses filtered human/mouse RefSeq and fly FlyBase references; exact-gene recovery requires matching one reference isoform's CDS. Overlapping transcripts are excluded from the modeled joint structures. [König et al. 2016, Methods and Results](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btw494)

With human RNA-seq evidence for both methods, Table 2 reports these human whole-genome percentages; Sp is the paper's prediction-specificity metric. [Journal Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5860283/#btw494-T2)

| Method | Gene Sn / Sp | Exon Sn / Sp |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AUGUSTUS | 52.43 / 37.31 | 81.69 / 81.65 |
| AUGUSTUS-CGP | 61.30 / 50.96 | 87.62 / 86.80 |

Table 3 reports aggregate CPU time for each entire twelve-genome clade: fly 20/28 CPU-days, vertebrates 1/1.6 CPU-years, for de novo/annotation-transfer versus RNA-seq modes. Peak memory per alignment chunk is respectively 4/4 GB and 5/10 GB. These are historical CGP totals, not per-genome wall times; alignment construction is not itemized there. [Journal Table 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5860283/#btw494-T3)

## MAKER2: arithmetic accuracy, evidence agreement, and cost scope

The [MAKER2 paper](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2105-12-491) defines accuracy as `(Sn + Sp) / 2`, where Sp has the prediction denominator. AED replaces the reference with clustered supporting alignments and equals `1 - (Sn + Sp) / 2`. **Inference:** AED measures evidence agreement; reuse of prediction evidence does not supply an independent truth endpoint.

The mismatched-parameter experiment applies worm parameters to Arabidopsis and Arabidopsis parameters to fly/worm, then adds species ESTs and protein evidence through MAKER2. It tests evidence-assisted recovery from poor parameter choice, not a universal DNA-only model. The reference regions are Arabidopsis chromosome 4, fly chromosome 3R and worm chromosome V. [Methods and Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3280279/)

Its scaling experiment actually annotated the 10-Mb nGASP worm sample, taking just under one hour on 32 cores of an AMD Opteron 6174 server. Whole-worm and human completion times are projections in that paper, not completed whole-genome runs. [Parallelization methods, results and Figure 5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3280279/)

I parsed supplementary Tables 1–2 and main Tables 1–2, computing arithmetic means with decimal arithmetic. All 72 main-table nucleotide/exon accuracy cells agree with the supplementary pairs within 0.005 percentage points. For the mismatched-parameter Arabidopsis SNAP example, the supplement reports exon Sn/Sp of 9.48/27.67 before MAKER2 and 46.86/73.35 within it. Their recomputed F1 values are 14.12% and 57.19%, whereas their arithmetic means are 18.575% and 60.105%. This checks published-data arithmetic; no predictions were generated. [Supplementary Tables 1–2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3280279/bin/1471-2105-12-491-S1.PDF)
