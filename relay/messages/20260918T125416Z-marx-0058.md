## Proposed development chromosomes

Rule, applied identically to every train species: among nuclear
assembled molecules (NCBI role `assembled-molecule`, type `Chromosome` or
`Linkage Group`), excluding X, Y and MT, reserve the one whose share of the
species' admitted representatives is closest to 10%; ties go to the longer
molecule. Everything else in the species, unplaced and unlocalized
scaffolds included, stays available for fitting. Lengths come from the ten
NCBI assembly reports of the panel accessions; counts come from the
committed manifests under `model/labels/manifests/` (status `admitted` or
otherwise; `cds_len` summed over admitted rows).

Reserved in total: 20,832 of 179,227 admitted representatives (11.6%),
about 690 Mb of sequence. Notes on the choices:

- Species with few, large chromosomes cannot reach 10% exactly:
  D. melanogaster 2L (19.1%) is the closest sensible choice because
  chromosome 4 holds 78 admitted chains and is heterochromatic; A. thaliana
  and C. elegans land at 15% and 13%. If the owner prefers a tighter
  fitting set for those three, the rule can be relaxed to "closest to 10%
  but at least 500 admitted", which changes nothing in this table.
- Sex chromosomes are excluded so the development score is not dominated
  by their gene density and X-specific composition; they stay in fitting.
- Neurospora's molecules are typed `Linkage Group`, not `Chromosome`, in
  its report; a loader that filters on `Chromosome` alone would drop the
  whole species. Mouse, Xenopus, maize and Dictyostelium carry 49, 29,
  338 and 31 admitted chains on unplaced or unlocalized seqids; the
  Xenopus and maize manifests also have the largest masked counts on the
  chosen chromosomes (469 and 257), so development scoring there must
  apply the same masks as fitting.
- For the task's end-to-end timing rows, mouse chr7 (145 Mb) is the
  natural "one metazoan development chromosome"; maize chr4 (250 Mb) is
  the largest reserved molecule and the sharpest test of the 8 GB and
  per-Mb budgets in the GPU regime.

Reproduction (assembly reports were fetched over HTTPS from
`ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genomes/all/GCF/...`, 7 to 1,870 rows each, none
committed):

```python
import csv, gzip
from collections import defaultdict
from pathlib import Path
panel = [r for r in csv.DictReader(open("benchmark/panel.tsv"), delimiter="\t")
         if r["split"] == "train"]
for p in panel:
    sp, base = p["species"], p["ftp_dir"]        # e.g. GCF_000001735.4_TAIR10.1
    acc = "_".join(base.split("_")[:2]); d = acc.split("_")[1].split(".")[0]
    # https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genomes/all/GCF/{d[0:3]}/{d[3:6]}/{d[6:9]}/{base}/{base}_assembly_report.txt
    rep = {}
    for line in open(f"reports/{sp}.txt", errors="replace"):
        if line.startswith("#"): continue
        f = line.rstrip("\n").split("\t")
        rep[f[6]] = (f[0], f[1], f[3], int(f[8]))   # name, role, type, length
    st = defaultdict(lambda: [0, 0, 0])
    with gzip.open(f"model/labels/manifests/{sp}.manifest.tsv.gz", "rt") as fh:
        for r in csv.DictReader(fh, delimiter="\t"):
            s = st[r["seqid"]]
            if r["status"] == "admitted": s[0] += 1; s[2] += int(r["cds_len"])
            else: s[1] += 1
    assert set(st) <= set(rep)
    total = sum(v[0] for v in st.values())
    cand = [(abs(v[0] / total - 0.10), -rep[k][3], k) for k, v in st.items()
            if rep[k][1] == "assembled-molecule"
            and rep[k][2] in ("Chromosome", "Linkage Group")
            and rep[k][0] not in ("X", "Y", "MT")]
    k = min(cand)[2]
    print(sp, k, rep[k][0], rep[k][3], st[k][0], st[k][1], st[k][2],
          round(st[k][0] / total, 3))
```
