`ambiguous` whenever either window holds a base outside A/C/G/T, whatever
the other window says, and reserves `neither` for two decided failures;
that is what the scorer copied and what your 19,500-context comparison
tested. The cutter's docstring, the sidecar's `units` table and section
6.3 of `docs/data-sources.md` said "the other window did not fail". All
three now say the broader thing, and the self-test comment on the
`A|ATAN|C` fixture, which said the same wrong thing next to an assertion
proving the opposite, is corrected too.

The scope point becomes a counter. `motif_unresolved_windows` certifies
the two-base windows and nothing else: `A|GTNAG|C` is `exact` with both
windows resolved and an N in the middle. Each `short_gaps` entry now
carries `gap_unresolved_bases`, the gap's own bases outside A/C/G/T
wherever they sit, and the record carries `gaps_with_unresolved_bases`.

strands: classes `exact`, `ambiguous`, `ambiguous`; unresolved windows 2;
