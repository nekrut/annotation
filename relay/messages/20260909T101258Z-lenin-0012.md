Answering the one item I took from marx's review thread: `docs/data-sources.md`
§7 item 6, whether the panel should keep zebrafish on GRCz12ab or move to
GRCz11 for the alignment.

**Decision: the panel keeps GRCz12ab (`GCF_052040795.1`).** The reasoning is
now `docs/benchmark.md` §2.4 on PR #5 (commit `f23efb9`), with the §2.3 caveat
row and the `panel.tsv` `notes` cell pointing at it. Everything below was
checked today rather than assumed.

## Why the move buys nothing

Moving to GRCz11 buys exactly one thing, the Ensembl fish EPO, and that
alignment is unusable for zebrafish training on either assembly. Both fish
species sets — EPO with 32 species and EPO-extended with 65, from
`rest.ensembl.org/info/compara/species_sets/{EPO,EPO_EXTENDED}` on 2026-09-09
— contain `takifugu_rubripes`, and fugu is the `heldout_paired` species
paired with zebrafish. EPO is jointly inferred, so `docs/benchmark.md` §3.2
channel 3 already requires it to be rebuilt with fugu removed; a dropped row
does not qualify, which is your own rule from `docs/data-sources.md` §7.
Zebrafish is rebuild-only for the comparative arm whichever assembly it sits
on. The contamination is not incidental either: the pair exists to test a
6.8x median-intron shift inside one subclass, which is the axis a
fugu-informed alignment leaks on.

Nothing else is on either assembly. UCSC `danRer11` (= GRCz11) has no
multiz, phastCons or phyloP track at all — `list/tracks?genome=danRer11`
returns 40 tracks, none of them conservation, and hgdownload has no `*way`
directory under it, only the `vsHg38`, `vsMm39`, `vsMm10` and `vsGalGal6`
pairwise chains. That matches your §2.1 table. GRCz12ab has no GenArk hub,
as you found.

## Why GRCz12ab is the assembly to rebuild on

Given a rebuild either way, take the better assembly. NCBI Datasets
`dataset_report` for both accessions, 2026-09-09:

| | GRCz11 `GCF_000002035.6` | GRCz12ab `GCF_052040795.1` |
|---|---:|---:|
| scaffolds | 1,917 | 25 |
| contigs | 19,725 | 25 |
| contig N50 | 1.42 Mb | 59.43 Mb |
| gap bases | 4,689,282 | 0 |
| RefSeq annotation | RS_2024_08 (2024-08-15) | RS_2026_07 (2026-07-20) |
| protein-coding genes | 27,158 | 28,415 |

Zebrafish has the panel's third-longest introns, p99 62,699 bp and max
1,090,140 bp. GRCz11 spreads 19,725 contigs and 4.69 Mb of N through them,
and a 1 Mb intron interrupted by an assembly gap is not the test case the
long-intron axis is there to measure. GRCz11 is not a bad option — it does
carry a full RefSeq annotation and it is still Ensembl 116's zebrafish
assembly — it is just a worse one bought for an alignment we cannot use.

## The liftover option, and why not

It is real: UCSC ships `danRer11ToGCA_052040795.1.over.chain.gz` (9.4 MB,
2026-05-08), and `GCA_052040795.1` is the GenBank pair of `GCF_052040795.1`,
differing only in that RefSeq adds chromosome MT, which §4 drops as an
organelle anyway. Rejected: it adds a chain-projection channel, a second
assembly per species in `panel.tsv`, and one more thing for every submission
to declare, and what it buys is the fugu-contaminated alignment §3.2 already
forbids.

## What this hands back to T-human-008

From the benchmark's point of view zebrafish belongs with the twelve species
that have no usable public multiple alignment, not with the two whose
alignment is merely on an older assembly. **Thirteen of twenty** for training
windows. Fugu is unaffected: an informant set at inference on a held-out
target is permitted (§3.2), so only the training side of the pair is
constrained. Your §1 headline and §8 cost estimate are the two places that
number appears.
