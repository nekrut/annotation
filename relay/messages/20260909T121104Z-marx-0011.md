## What changed on `work/T-human-008-marx` (commit 219243c, PR #8)

1. **The hg38 470-way is denser than I said, not sparser.** New `scripts/data/tree_composition.py` (stdlib Newick parser, patristic distance from the reference, per-clade table) with `scripts/data/hg38.470way.orders.tsv` (order and family per leaf from GBIF, 2026-09-09). The 470-way is mammal-only: 413 of its 469 informants are within 0.5 substitutions per site of human, 359 of them packed into the 0.25 to 0.5 band (126 artiodactyls, 87 rodents, 68 carnivores, 52 bats), and only platypus and echidna are beyond 1.0. Section 6.2 item 2 had predicted the 470-way would have a smaller in-horizon fraction than the 100-way (65 of 99); it now says the informant axis must handle both a mostly-unaligned regime (vertebrate-wide and fly trees) and a dense, near-redundant one (mammal tree), which favours subsampling or clade-pooling rows at cut time on the dense side. The script reproduces the section 6.1 human 100-way histogram exactly (9, 2, 40, 14, 24, 10), which is the check on the parser.
2. **470 leaves are 431 species.** 35 species appear as two or more assemblies or subspecies (two dog assemblies plus dingo, two mouse, two rat, two jaguar, and so on), about 8% of rows. Relevant to anyone counting informants for T-human-011.
3. **The 470-way does have raw MAF.** My table said "no maf.gz directory"; there is no gzipped copy, but `goldenPath/hg38/multiz470way/maf/` serves 499 uncompressed per-chromosome files, 5.9 TB in total (chr1 alone 471 GB). Corrected in section 2.1 and section 9 item 7: for this track the bigMaf API and Range reads are the only laptop-scale paths and a full copy is a cluster storage request. Distances on the 470-way and 100-way trees agree to about 10% (mouse 0.547 versus 0.502, dog 0.371 versus 0.332).
