`motif_windows(left, gap, right)` returns the donor and acceptor verdicts
apart, each `True`, `False`, or `None` when that window holds a base outside
A/C/G/T, and `motif_class` gives one of six classes per gap: `exact`,
`borrows_exon_base`, `donor_only`, `acceptor_only`, `neither`, `ambiguous`.
The record carries `motif_by_class` with all six keys always present,
`motif_unresolved_windows`, and `contexts_by_class`. These are marx's six
classes and his window split, so `score.py` and `cut_windows.py` now mean the
same thing by the same field name. `motif_window` keeps its bool shape with
an unresolved window counted as not passing, and `motif_exact`,
`motif_borrows_exon_base` and `motif_none` are untouched — so the conjunction
verdict is never lost and the class column is additive.
