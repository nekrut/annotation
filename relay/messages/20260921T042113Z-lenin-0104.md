- `fast_viterbi.scan_segments` / `viterbi_segments`: a batch of *segments*
  is scanned `tile` bases at a time and a `Carry` crosses each seam
  (`alpha`, `tau`, the residual-intron layer, the `<= m - 1` pending donors
  with the matching seam-context emissions so their mandatory windows are
  re-read from the same operands, the edge-mode running score / final,
  the tails a donor has entered so far, and the rows already ended). Only
  one tile of operands is alive; the back-pointers of a whole segment are
  still held (120 B per base at `R = 3`). Interior seams get no partials
  (proposal 3.1 kept); `edges` applies to the two real ends only.
- Parity: scores bit-identical to the unbroken scan, `exit_r` / `entered`
  identical inside every row, every traceback identical
  (`tests/test_a_fast_viterbi.py::SeamCarry`: 200 random batches of 1–6
  segments at tiles 1 / 2 / 3 / 5 / 7 / 11 / 64, `m` 1–4, `R` 1–3, codes
  1 / 6 / alternative initiators, `-inf` masks, with and without an
  `EdgePrior`; > 400 finite cases, > 100 with a chain across a seam).
  `test_a_chromosome` adds one segment in 50-base tiles with both genes
  crossing a tile seam, two overlapping segments, and tiles of 7. 189
  tests pass under 3.11 + torch.
- `chromosome.predict_sequence(segments=)` / `measure --segments N`: each
  strand cut into about `N` segments overlapping by `--overlap`, encoded
  in non-overlapping 12,288-base tiles on the anchored pooling grid,
  all segments of both strands decoded in one batch.

Reading: the per-step fixed cost (~37 µs) makes the exact two-row decode
5.5× the batch-64 window decode, so the batch has to be filled with
segments and each segment seam brings back the overlap; at 19 segments the
batch equals the overlap-0 window row's and the decode stage is the same
1.57 s, the encoder carries the 1.32× oversampling, and the row is inside
15 on all three numerators with the 4,096-base containment guarantee kept
at segment seams and every interior tile seam exact. The oversampling is
`1 + (S − 1) × 4,096 / n`: 1.32 on yeast chr I, 1.004 at 20 Mb, where the
window mode's 1.5× is fixed — the metazoan row is where the gap shows, and
there the held back-pointers (4.8 GB at 40 M oriented bases) force the
packed store (~35 B per base) before the 8 GB cap. The segment rows still
differ from the exact decode by 6–11 chains, partly because the encoder
has no context past a tile edge (the tile grid moves with the segment
count); an encoder context margin is the cheap fix and belongs with the
packing step. No B allowance claimed: one yeast chromosome, a 20-step
checkpoint, free grammar at the real strand ends, S. pombe unmeasured.
