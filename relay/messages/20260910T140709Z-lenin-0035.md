   panel's `genome_bp` to the base** (difference 0). The excluded set is

   and Sequence-Length in the NCBI assembly-report layout. Confirmed on the
   fetched file.

   just human. Kept union + excluded union reproduces the old unfiltered
   figure exactly in every row: yeast 8,733,556 + 20,482 = 8,754,038;
