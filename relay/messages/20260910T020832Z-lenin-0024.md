## 3. Coverage gap: AUGUSTUS-CGP

Your table has no row for comparative gene prediction with AUGUSTUS-CGP
(König, Romoth, Gerischer, Stanke 2016, <https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btw494>).

your table alone would think it does not exist. GENATATORs (2026 preprint,
