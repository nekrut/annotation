Landed the differentiable PyTorch chain loss for candidate A on
`work/T-human-014-lenin` (commit 69a0c43, [PR #38](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/pull/38)),
the section-2 item both reviewers have been tracking as pending.

`model/a/torch_loss.py` `chain_nll(x, emissions, cds_ranges, intron_ranges)`
returns `log Z − log Z_num` on a `(11, n)` emission tensor (channel order
identical to `model.grammar.Scores.channels()` and the encoder's `[B, 11, L]`
head), computed with `torch.logsumexp`. Autograd therefore yields
`dL/de = P_free(e) − P_num(e)`, the CRF marginal difference a training step
applies to the emission head.

It is **parity-by-construction**: the forward reuses the *reviewed* grammar
state machine unchanged — `ReferenceDecoder.initial`/`transitions`/`terminal`
over a `TorchScores` view of the emission tensor — so the only substitution
against the standard-library oracle in `model/a/loss.py` is Python-float
`+`/`logsumexp` for torch `+`/`torch.logsumexp`. `support_mask` builds the
additive `-inf`/`0` mask that reproduces `numerator_scores`.

`tests/test_a_torch_loss.py` (torch-gated, 9 cases) pins: the mask matches the
oracle channel-for-channel; the loss value matches the oracle on the
section-3.4 single- and two-exon fixtures with zero and with non-zero
emissions; the `log 2` zero-emission case; `loss >= 0`; and that autograd's
gradient at the gold start base equals the oracle's finite-difference marginal
difference (bounded in (-1, 0]).

Scope and honesty: this forward has the *reference* recurrence's cost (explicit
mandatory-intron states, a Python dict per boundary), so at the default `m=20`
it is unsuitable for a full training window. It is the differentiable
**reference** the fast vectorized delayed-entry kernel will be checked against —
the same relation `DelayedEntryDecoder` has to `ReferenceDecoder` — not the
training-throughput kernel, and it does not by itself unblock the end-to-end
measurement. Complete targets only: an edge-enabled decoder is rejected, as in
the oracle; edge-partial numerators remain the section-3.6 boundary increment.

This is a work-product increment, not an acceptance request. T-human-014 and its
unmeasured budget stay open pending the fast kernel, a runnable training entry
point, and the gagarin fitting/inference run. Reviews welcome. Local: 60
