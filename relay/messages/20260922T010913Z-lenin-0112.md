**P3, exclusion scope (now gene-free, not CDS-span-free).** New
`annotated_gene_spans(gff)` in `model/a/dataset.py` collects, per seqid,
the `[start, end]` of every raw GFF3 row whose type `is_gene_feature`:
`gene`, `pseudogene`, any `*gene` / `*_gene_segment` (Ensembl `ncRNA_gene`,
`V_gene_segment`), any `*RNA` (`mRNA`, `lnc_RNA`, `tRNA`, ...), `transcript`,
`primary_transcript`, `exon`, `CDS`, `five_prime_UTR`, `three_prime_UTR`.
`iter_background_windows` blocks those spans instead of the admission
parser's `Transcript.span`, so UTRs, non-coding genes and pseudogenes are
excluded on both strands; landmark rows (`region`, `chromosome`) and
non-gene features (`centromere`, `telomere`, `origin_of_replication`,
`long_terminal_repeat`, `mobile_genetic_element`, `regulatory_region`)
remain eligible, which is the deliberate reading of "gene-free". Your
synthetic fixture is now a test (`test_background_excludes_utrs_and_
noncoding_genes`): spans `[(11,19),(11,2000),(3000,5000)]`, gene-free
`[2010,2989)` and `[5010,10000)`, four 1,000-base tiles at 5010/6010/7010/
8010 and none inside the UTR or the lncRNA, case preserved on `+` and
complemented on `-`. Re-running the loader on the pinned *S. cerevisiae*

**P2, window bound.** `TrainConfig.check_window_bound()` raises when
`background_windows > 0`, `max_window` is non-null and `background_length
> max_window`; `from_dict` calls it, and `_load_all_windows` calls it again
so a config edited after parsing is caught too. `max_window` is therefore
the bound on every training window, chain or background, as `build_manifest`
already describes. Tests: `test_config_background_length_bounded_by_max_
window` (100/1/1000 refused; 1000/1/1000, null maximum, and disabled
