**engels-0039**

1. *Partial edge.* Each declared end is now checked at its own edge in
   transcriptional orientation (5' end: position 1 on plus, last base on minus;
   3' end the reverse), and a chain declaring both must satisfy both. The four
   wrong-edge fixtures are masked `partial_5,partial_away_from_edge` or
   `partial_3,partial_away_from_edge`; the same declarations at their own edge
   and a both-ends chain spanning the whole sequence are admitted. An interior
   range declaration no longer sets `partial_5`/`partial_3`; it masks the chain
   as `partial_unlocated` and makes the junction at that row unknown.
2. *Auxiliary reliability.* Every benchmark-accepted transcript is FASTA-audited,
   keyed on its full identity. The summary now carries
   `auxiliary_sites_annotated`, `auxiliary_sites_retained` and
   `auxiliary_sites_unknown`; a site is retained only when some transcript
   observes it reliably (end not partial or unlocated, coordinates in range,
   initiator or stop present on that transcript's own spliced sequence; a
   junction unless an interior partial sits there). Your alternative-start
   fixture gives 2 annotated / 1 retained / 1 unknown; the internal-partial
   fixture gives donor 0 retained / 1 unknown with the acceptor retained.
3. *Transcript-level `transl_except`* masks like one on a CDS row.
4. *Codon features.* `gff3_rows` emits `start_codon` and `stop_codon` after the
   CDS rows, split across introns when the codon is (each piece with the GFF3
   phase of its continuation, e.g. T|AA gives phases 0 and 2), omitted on a
   censored end, terminal stop kept inside the CDS; `codon_features` returns
   them as `Feature`s. Tested unsplit, split in both positions for initiator and
   stop, re-read on both strands, and on 5'- and 3'-partial chains.
