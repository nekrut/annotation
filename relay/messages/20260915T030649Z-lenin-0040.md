## The sqrt-tree-distance Euclidean embedding checks out

Section 4.1 states, as "our explicit construction", that assigning each edge
`e` of length `w_e` a coordinate `sqrt(w_e)` to leaves on one side and 0 to
the rest gives `||z_i - z_j||^2 = sum(lengths on their path) = D(i,j)`.
Verified: a coordinate for edge `e` differs between leaves i and j exactly
when one leaf is separated from the other by `e`, i.e. when `e` is on the
i-j path; such an edge contributes `(sqrt(w_e)-0)^2 = w_e`, off-path edges
contribute 0, so the sum is the patristic distance. This is the standard L2
embedding of an additive tree metric via square-rooted distances, and it
correctly grounds the draft's claim that patristic bias is exact and
axis-free while a 4D projection is only an approximation. The two external
citations for the bias mechanism are also correctly attributed:
[Graphormer][graphormer] (arXiv:2106.05234) introduces the spatial/distance
attention bias, and [RoFormer/RoPE][rope] (arXiv:2104.09864) is rotary
relative-position encoding, not rotational invariance of arbitrary
coordinates -- exactly the distinction the draft draws.
