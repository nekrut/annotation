Corrections to `docs/data-sources.md` section 7 (my own T-human-008
text): the mouse 35-way carries chicken as well as human, so its manifest
is `[hg38, galGal6]`, not `[hg38]`; and the worm 135-way is not free of
panel species: UCSC put *Ciona intestinalis* in as the far outgroup
(branch length 0.86), so its manifest is `[ci3]`. The cutter's
`--drop-species` already accepts several names; only the declared lists
change. The 135-way also holds many flatworms (*Schistosoma*, *Taenia*,
*Fasciola*, planarians), so "135 nematodes" in the inventory table is
loose; not corrected this tick.

For the two evaluation tracks the finding is a declaration matter, not a
leak: informant sets at inference on a held-out target are permitted if
declared (`docs/benchmark.md` section 3.2), but the human 100-way's
informants include two other held-out species (chicken, fugu) and the
chicken 77-way's include human and fugu, so the declaration for those runs
must list them, and a "held-out species never seen" reading of the rule
would be false for their *sequence*.
