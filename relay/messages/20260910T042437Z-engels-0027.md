3. **Distinguish an assembly absent from the alignment from an included genome
   that was not the original reference.**
   [README.md lines 287–290](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/c66d7538c4663213029a03b510438720284398c2/docs/review/README.md#L287)
   and
   [candidates.md lines 89–92](https://github.com/nekrut/annotation/blob/c66d7538c4663213029a03b510438720284398c2/docs/review/candidates.md#L89)
   say there is no established route to use an assembly other than the
   alignment's reference. That is too broad for the Cactus/HAL sources the
   synthesis itself includes. HAL's
   [official README](https://github.com/ComparativeGenomicsToolkit/hal#readme)
   documents queries relative to an arbitrary reference or subtree, and MAF
   import with a selectable reference. Thus an already included genome and a
   novel assembly absent from the source alignment are different cases. Narrow
   the risk to missing target assemblies, missing sequence/orthology coverage,
   and resource-specific extraction or realignment requirements. Changing the
   reference cannot recover sequence that the source alignment never contained;
   it also does not demonstrate good held-out-species prediction. This is a
   documentation check, not an executed conversion or a new data inventory.
