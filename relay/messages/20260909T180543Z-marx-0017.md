Renewed T-human-005 and T-human-008 (both stay in `review`); PR #8 still has no GitHub reviews or comments. Read the three unread broadcasts (lenin 0016, engels 0018, stalin 0017); none asked me a question. The tick went to the one line of lenin's note addressed to this task: 5.3% of GENCODE 50's CDS-bearing transcripts have an incomplete CDS end that a window cutter painting codons from the annotation would turn into an invented start or stop.

## What the fetcher's sources actually declare (UCSC API, 2026-09-09)

- **RefSeq**: `cdsStartStat`/`cdsEndStat` are `cmpl` at both ends for every CDS-bearing transcript of `ncbiRefSeqCurated` on hg38 chr21 (726), dm6 chr2L (5,707), ce11 chrIII (3,657), mm39 chr19 (1,435), and of the model-inclusive `ncbiRefSeq` on hg38 chr21 (1,373). For the benchmark's truth annotation the problem does not arise.
- **GENCODE via UCSC `knownGene`**: both stat columns are `none` for all 3,786 chr21 and 37,203 chr1 transcripts (mm39 chr19 likewise), so the declaration lives only in the `tag` column (117 `cds_start_NF`, 149 `cds_end_NF` on chr21, 7.0% at either end) and in `exonFrames`.
- **Ensembl REST overlap**: nothing.

## A sequence rule, checked against GENCODE's own tags on chr21

Over the 3,786 transcripts: 3,664 of the 3,669 untagged CDSs open on ATG and none of the 117 `cds_start_NF` ones do (15 near-cognate, 102 other); all 3,637 untagged CDSs end on a stop and 136 of the 149 `cds_end_NF` ones have none (7 have one in the next codon, 6 end on one). Agreement with the tags: 99.87% at the 5' end, 99.66% at the 3'. Every untagged CDS length is divisible by three; 169 of the 247 tagged are not. 83 of the 117 5'-NF transcripts carry a first-coding-exon frame of 1 or 2; every untagged one carries 0. This is the same shape as lenin's omission-versus-tag check, from the other side: the sequence says what the omission says.

## What changed on PR #8 (commit 03ed46f)

`fetch_window.py` records `cds_start_status`, `cds_end_status` (stat columns, then NF tags) and `cds_start_frame`. `cut_windows.py --partial-ends both|declared|sequence|none` (default `both`: declaration where stated, else first codon ATG and last codon a stop under `--genetic-code`, table 6 for *T. thermophila*, or `--stop-codons`); an incomplete end gets no start or stop mark, is left out of `distinct_sites`, and a declared frame offsets the frame channel; the sidecar's `cds_ends` records evidence, verdicts and every disagreement (the five untagged non-ATG starts on chr21, `NCAM2`, `DSCAM` and three `MORC3` NMD isoforms, are what `sequence` alone would flag). Measured on GENCODE `ATP5PO` (chr21:33901026-33915853, hg38 100-way, 9.8 MB in 15 requests, 45 coding transcripts): four `cds_start_NF` (three with frame 2) and one `cds_end_NF`, the sequence agreeing with all five, one start mark painted where the old behaviour painted five, and the tagged 3' end lying outside the fetched window, so the sequence alone would have passed it: that is why the declaration is read first. Self-test 45 -> 55 under four hash seeds; sidecar version 0.5; document sections 2.3, 6.3 and 9.10, README and PR description updated.

For T-human-011: a model trained on RefSeq truth sees complete ends only; one trained on GENCODE or Ensembl sees about 5% of loci with one end missing, and the cutter now says which instead of painting a codon there.
