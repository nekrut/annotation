## Label contract and raw-row audit

Section 3.6 specifies how the first structured-chain loss handles partial
ends, phase/row incompatibility, translation exceptions and ambiguous CDS.
Interior partial ends do not acquire sequence-edge inference permissions.
Excluded full spans are unconstrained in the numerator; reliable auxiliary
sites remain independently supervised. Benchmark reference denominators
are unchanged.

The accepted scorer merges overlapping/adjacent CDS intervals, so section
4.4's reproduction now also retains raw rows and attributes for metadata
checks. On the same four MD5-verified train GFFs, after topology masking,
6 / 28 / 455 / 102 representatives (yeast / worm / fly / mouse) have at
least one exclusion/review flag. The unflagged remainders are
5,858 / 19,811 / 12,980 / 21,711; these are not sequence-validated intact
CDS counts. The flag table includes nonedge partials, internal range tags,
exception/translation tags, short gaps, overlapping rows, and terminal or
initial frame concerns. Flags overlap. No FASTA or held-out annotation
was used. NCBI GFF3 semantics are linked in the artifact.

## Candidate C now has explicit resource terms

Sections 5.1–5.3 replace the 1.20 M reservation with a 577-scalar edge
head, or 495,598 total with B's shared components. Typed endpoint caps and
eight top-four range queries give E<=32V without scoring every possible
pair first. This is approximate pruning, with separate required vertex,
edge and complete-path survival audits.

The edge count does not make grammar checking O(E): the specified direct
exon scans cost O(P*W), where W counts repeated exon-edge bases. Path
bookkeeping, emitted block lists, feature setup and scratch reads also
remain visible. C's small MLP is not an end-to-end speed claim. Resource
limits and actual candidate recall await the authorized Phase 4 pilot.
