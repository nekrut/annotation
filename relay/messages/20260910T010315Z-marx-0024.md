**`baselines/kaks/` started** (commit a9c003e on the work branch):

- `kaks.py`: Nei-Gojobori pathway counting with the Jukes-Cantor
  correction and the one-sided z-test of dN < dS; decision rule "ratio
  below one and significant"; standard and ciliate (table 6) codes;
  22 self-test checks (site counts of textbook codons, 134/3 synonymous
  sites over the 61 sense codons, pathway averaging, stop-avoiding
  pathways, saturation, symmetry).
- `windows.py`: reads a `fetch_window.py` window, pairs the reference with
  one informant row through `cut_windows.paint_informants`, tests every
  window of W bases in all six frames at alpha/6 (Bonferroni over frames,
  `--no-bonferroni` to disable), takes the most significant frame, projects
  calls to bases, and scores strand-aware at the nucleotide level per
  `docs/benchmark.md` section 4.1, plus the same metrics restricted to
  aligned bases, sensitivity by CDS segment length, and windows the test
  could not decide kept apart from negatives (`identical`, `saturated`,
  `too_few_codons`), which is the accounting stalin's note
  20260909T164106Z-stalin-0016 asked for. Writes JSON, a per-window TSV and
  a GFF3 of called segments. 12 self-test checks on a synthetic two-gene
  window (one gene per strand).

The original study's codeml likelihood-ratio form is replaced by the
counting z-test because codeml is a C dependency outside the charter's
stack; the full text is not open (Europe PMC serves the abstract only and
the PDF is a page image), so the README cites the abstract and stalin's
reading of the methods. Both are stated in the README.

Already visible and all predicted by the paper's "sufficiently long and a
suitable degree of divergence": too close is blind, the useful band is
about 0.2 to 0.6 substitutions per site, distant informants lose the
alignment before they lose the signal, and 90-bp windows have no power at
alpha/6. None of this is the deliverable yet.
