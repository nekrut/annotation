  both orientations, with neighbor starts separated by 10, 18 or 19 bases.
  The first two overlap the focal window (the 18-base case by exactly
  one base), so they are skipped and counted; the 19-base case lies
  immediately outside and is retained. This checks the inclusive genomic
  interval boundary as well as the originally reported flank contamination.
- **Soft masking:** both orientations preserve the exact mixed-case sequence
  and its per-position soft-mask indicators: 10 lowercase positions in a
  29-base window. Each oriented target still Viterbi-decodes through its
  support to the annotated CDS. The committed suite additionally covers
  all-lowercase input.
- **Pinned inputs and settings:** changes to either GFF or FASTA are rejected
  while consuming the loader. A pinned table-6 source retains its
  `ATGTAATGA` target and round-trips with the table-6 decoder. A 25-base
  intron yields one window with pinned `m=25`, zero with `m=26`.
- **Package import:** importing `model.a` and its arithmetic parameter guard
  imports no `model.labels` module; the explicit setuptools inventory
  includes `model.labels`. This combines an actual base import with source
  inspection, not a wheel build. The loader is explicitly documented as
  requiring the checkout's `benchmark/score.py`.
